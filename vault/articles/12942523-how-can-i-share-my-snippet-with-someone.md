You can share a snippet in two ways: **by copying the content** or **by mentioning someone in a comment**.

## Share by copying

#### **[[articles/11775995-copy-all-your-snippets-with-comments|Copy all snippets from a Topic]] (with comments):**

-   Go to your **Dashboard**

-   Open the **Topic** you want to copy from

-   Click the **three dots** in the top right corner

-   Select **Copy Snippets**

-   Paste the content into any document (Google Docs, Word, Notion, etc.)

Formatting and comments are included.

#### **[[articles/11783170-copy-content-from-a-single-snippet|Copy a single snippet:]]**

-   Find the snippet

-   Click the **three dots**

-   Select **Copy Content**

-   Paste it wherever you need

## Share by mentioning someone in a comment (@)

**Inside a Topic:**

When you mention someone in a comment, they receive **an email** with a link to the snippet and get access to the Topic based on the role you assign (Viewer, Commenter, Editor, or [[articles/11830845-mention-only|Mention only]])

> [!warning]
> **_[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|👉🏼 How to mention collaborators in comments]]_**

**Inside your Inbox:**
You can’t add collaborators to your Inbox, but you _can_ mention someone by their email under a snippet if you need their input:

-   They will receive **that specific snippet by email**

-   They will be able to view it on the **original source**

-   They **won’t see the rest of your Inbox**

> [!note]
> 📌 **Note:** Collaborators cannot be added to the Inbox.
