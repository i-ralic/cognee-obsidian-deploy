When this option is enabled, the Cluing Extension will open **automatically on every page you visit**. You don’t need to manually launch it each time.

## **How to** always have the extension open while browsing:

1.  Open the Cluing Extension in your browser

2.  Click on the **Settings** button in the top-right corner

3.  Find the **Keep Open on Every Page** option

4.  Toggle it on
    ​
