The Clu.ing link is a unique page that brings all your sources and snippets together **with the purpose of sharing your insight with others**.

You can find the link **at the bottom** of the Cluing extension or by clicking the share button on any Topic in your Dashboard.

![](/media/2509343844/image.webp)

Once you copy the link, others can open it, and based on [[articles/11792079-sharing-options|the permissions you’ve set]], they can **view**, **comment**, or **edit** your snippets.

![](/media/2509342466/image.webp)
