## Accessing Kindle

To easily access your Kindle notes, you can do it both through the extension and your dashboard. Here’s how:

## **Via Extension**

-   Open the Cluing **extension**.

-   Click the **Settings icon** in the top right corner.

-   From the list, select **Kindle Book Notes**.

-   Once you click, your Kindle notes will open.

-   Open the Extension again and start importing you highlights

![](/media/2525669935/image.webp)

## Via Dashboard

-   Go to your **Dashboard**.

-   Open any **Topic**.

-   Click the **Quick Add button**.

-   Select **Import from Kindle**.

![](/media/2525679449/image.webp)

Your **Kindle notes** will open, and you can start importing your highlights directly into Cluing.
