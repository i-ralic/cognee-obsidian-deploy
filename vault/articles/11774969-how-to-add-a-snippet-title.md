For better organization, you can add Titles to your snippets. This makes it easier to recognize what you’ve saved at a glance.

## Add a Snippet Title from the Dashboard

1.  Open the **Topic** where your snippet is saved.

2.  Click the **three dots** on the snippet.

3.  Select **“Add Title”** from the menu.

4.  Type your Title and press **Enter** to save.

## Add a Snippet Title from the Extension

1.  Open the **Extension** and find the snippet.

2.  Click the **three dots** on the snippet.

3.  Select **“Add Title”** from the menu.

4.  Type your Title and press **Enter** to save.

<video src="/media/2519356487/snippet-title-exten.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="1022" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

## Snippet Title Suggestion

When adding a snippet, Cluing will automatically **suggest a title**, generated by AI based on the webpage’s title.

You can click to use the suggested title or easily **edit it** before saving.
