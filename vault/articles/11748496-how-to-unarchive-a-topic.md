## Unarchiving a Topic from the Extension

There are 5 steps to unarchiving a topic while browsing and using the extension:

1.  Open the extension

2.  Open the dropdown menu and find the **Archive Cluster**.

3.  Expand it to see the archived topics.

4.  Find the topic you want to unarchive - you’ll see the **Unarchive** button next to its name.

5.  Click it, and the topic will automatically return to the cluster it was in before being archived.

![](/media/2003520571/V7AlHYOZCQ.webp)

## Unarchiving a Topic from the Dashboard

To unarchive a topic while using the Dashboard, you'll need to:

1.  In the left sidebar on your Dashboard, find the **Archive Cluster** all the way at the bottom.

2.  Expand it and select the topic you want to unarchive.

3.  In the top right corner, click the three dots (**More options**).

4.  Choose **Unarchive**.

Unarchived topics go back to where they were created.
