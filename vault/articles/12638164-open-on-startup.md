**Choose what opens by default when you launch the Cluing extension.**

Under **Open on Startup**, you can choose what the extension should open by default:

-   **Inbox** - Opens your Inbox, a quick-drop space where you can save snippets instantly and organize them later.

-   **My Last Viewed Topic** - Reopens the last topic you were working on, helping you pick up right where you left off.

-   **Newest Topic on this Link** - Opens the most recently created Topic for the current page, or if none exists, it opens **the oldest one** available.

This way, you can set up the extension to match your workflow and save time.

![](/media/2219369279/LastViewdTopic.webp)

## Open Inbox on Startup

If you want the extension to automatically open your **Inbox** every time you launch the extension, follow these steps:

1.  **Open the Extension** - Click the Cluing icon in your browser.

2.  **Go to Settings** - Click the **Settings** button in the top-right corner.

3.  Scroll to the **Open on Startup** section.

4.  **Click “Inbox”** - Your Inbox will now open automatically whenever you launch the extension

> [!warning]
> ✅ This way, you can immediately see all your saved and new snippets.

## Open Your Last Viewed Topic on Startup

If you want the extension to reopen the last topic you were working on:

1.  **Open the Extension** - Click the Cluing icon.

2.  **Go to Settings** - Click the **Settings** button in the top-right corner.

3.  **Find “Open on Startup”** - Go to the **Open on Startup** section.

4.  **Click “My Last Viewed Topic”** - The extension will automatically open the topic you last viewed.

> [!warning]
> ✅ Now, every time you open the extension, you’ll pick up right where you left off, without any extra clicks.

## Open the Newest Topic on This Link on Startup

If you want the extension to open the most recent topic for the current page:

1.  **Open the Extension** - Click the Cluing icon.

2.  **Go to Settings** - Click the **Settings** button in the top-right corner.

3.  **Find “Open on Startup”** - Locate the **Open on Startup** section.

4.  **Click “Newest Topic on This Link”** - The extension will open the last created topic on that particular page.

    -   If no new topics exist, it will open the last active one.

> [!warning]
> ✅ This ensures you always start with the most up-to-date content.
