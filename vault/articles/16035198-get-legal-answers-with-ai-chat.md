AI Chat can help with legal research. Ask a law-related question and it provides a clear answer along with relevant **case-law results** you can open and verify directly.

> [!tip]
> **Note:** For deeper analysis, switch to the Legal Consultant agent, built for professional legal guidance.

## **How to get legal answers**

1.  Open **AI Chat** from the sidebar

2.  Select the **Legal Consultant** agent for more thorough legal analysis (Optional)

3.  Ask your legal-research question

4.  Read the answer and click any **case-law result** to open the source

> [!danger]
> ⁉️ AI Chat provides research and general legal information - it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified attorney.
