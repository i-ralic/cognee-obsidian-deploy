You can only manage Workspace Settings if you're the owner.

To access settings, navigate to the workspace panel in the top left corner and then click the “**Settings and members**” item in the workspace panel.

![](/media/1920774721/image.webp)

## Rename the workspace

To rename the workspace, just click on the name field and click **"Enter"** once you're done making changes.

![](/media/1920774018/image.webp)
