You can export your entire Topic, **including all its snippets**, as a CSV file.

Here's how:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Open the Topic you want to export as a CSV file

3.  Click on the **three dots** in the Topic menu

4.  Click on ,,**Download CSV**''

This lets you open your research in tools like **Google Sheets** or **Excel**, where you can easily sort, filter, and analyze the data.

![](/media/2499508700/image.webp)

It’s a simple way to save your content in an organized, editable format - perfect if you need to take your research outside of Cluing.
