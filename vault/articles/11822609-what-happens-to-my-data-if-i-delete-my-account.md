> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/14115869-security-and-privacy|Security and Privacy]]

When you delete your Cluing account, all data from your personal workspace is **immediately deleted**. Your email address is also removed from any third-party solutions we use for newsletters and support.

For content within a **Team Workspace**, your personal data (such as name, last name, and email) is anonymized, ensuring that the content you've collected and comments you've left are preserved for the team's continuity, while all your personal identifiable information is securely removed.
