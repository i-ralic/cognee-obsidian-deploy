## **How to access your Workspace**

When you create or are added to a Team Version, you’ll be able to see it in the top left corner of the navigation.

![](/media/2502262967/image.webp)

By **clicking on the arrows**, you’ll be able to see all workspaces that you are a member of and switch between them as needed.

## **Workspace Settings**

You can only edit Workspace Settings if you're **the owner**.

To access settings, navigate to the workspace panel **in the top left corner** and then click the “**Settings and members**” item in the workspace panel.
​

![](/media/2502265250/image.webp)

Here you can rename the workspace, [[articles/11791407-how-to-add-people-to-a-workspace|add additional team members]], and [[articles/11785769-how-to-delete-a-workspace|delete your workspace]].

## **How to rename the workspace**

To [[articles/11785971-how-to-rename-the-workspace|rename the workspace]], just click on the name field and click "**Enter**" once you're done making changes.

## **How to add users to your workspace**

To [[articles/11791407-how-to-add-people-to-a-workspace|add a new user to your workspace]], enter their Cluing account email in the 'Email Address' field and click the envelope on the right.

The user will then be added to your workspace.

![](/media/1804913809/image.webp)

If they don't have an account already, they'll be sent an **email invitation to join**.
​

> [!warning]
> **⚠️ Be aware that this option is only available with the Team Version plan!**
