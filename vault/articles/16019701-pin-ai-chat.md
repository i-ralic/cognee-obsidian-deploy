Pinned conversations are always sorted to the top of your AI Chat list.

## **How to pin a conversation**

1.  Open AI Chat History

2.  Click the **3dot menu** next to the conversation

3.  Select **Pin**

The conversation moves to the top of your list and stays there until you unpin it.

> [!warning]
> 📌 You can pin multiple conversations - they stay grouped at the top for quick access.
