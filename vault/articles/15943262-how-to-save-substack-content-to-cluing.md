With Cluing, you can capture any Substack post or note, whether you read it on the web/app or receive it in your inbox - and turn it into an organized snippet inside your workspace.

## Save from the Web

This is the fastest way if you're reading a Substack post in your browser.

1.  Open the Substack post you want to save

2.  Open **[[articles/11839482-how-to-install-the-cluing-extension-and-create-your-first-topic|Cluing extension]]**

3.  Choose how you want to capture it:

    -   **Highlight text** sand save it as **Snippet**

    -   Click **Bookmark** to save the whole post

    -   Click **[[articles/14211688-summarize-webpage|Summarize]]** to have Cluing generate a summary of the entire post

> [!tip]
> 📖 **Read the full post inside Cluing:** Once your Substack snippet is saved, open it and choose **"[[articles/12267724-cluing-reader#h_840ba47cfc|Open in Reader]]"** - the entire post will be displayed right inside Cluing.

## Auto-Save Substack Newsletters via Email

If you subscribe to a Substack via Cluing email, you can route those posts straight into Cluing so they never clutter your inbox.

1.  Open your **[[articles/12701607-how-to-manage-a-cluing-workspace|workspace settings]]** and go to the **Emails** section.

2.  Click to **add a new email** and choose the **Topic**

    1.  Cluing generates a **unique email address** for that Topic

3.  Use that address to **subscribe to the Substack**, or **forward** existing Substack emails to it

> [!tip]
> 📥 Check this article to see how to **[[articles/14058819-organize-your-newsletters-with-cluing|Organize Your Newsletters with Cluing]]**

## Save on Mobile

On mobile, you can save Substack posts, Notes, and full articles directly to Cluing. Here's how:

1.  Install the **[[articles/13833323-cluing-mobile-apps|Cluing mobile app]]** (iOS and Android)

2.  Open the Substack post and tap the **Share** button

3.  Select **Cluing** from the list of apps

    1.  Cluing generates a snippet - choose a Topic, edit the title, add a comment, and tap **Create**

> [!warning]
> **💡 The author will be attached to the snippet automatically.**
