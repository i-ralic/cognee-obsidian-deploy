## Exporting Your Topics and Snippets from Cluing

Cluing gives you **several ways** to take your saved insights outside the app - whether you need a quick copy-paste, a structured file, or a shareable document.

Here's an overview of all available export options.

## Download as CSV - Full Topic Export

Export an entire Topic, along with all its snippets, as a structured CSV file.

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Open the Topic you want to export

3.  Click the **three dots** in the Topic menu

4.  Select **"Download CSV"**

The file opens in **Google Sheets** or **Excel**, where you can sort, filter, and analyze your data.

![](/media/2499486037/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> → **_Best for_**_: working with your research in a spreadsheet, creating reports, or keeping a structured backup._

##
Print or Save as PDF - Topic as a Document

Turn any Topic into a clean PDF, with all snippets and comments included.

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Open the Topic you want to export

3.  Click **"More options"** (three dots) in the top right corner

4.  Choose **"Print"**

5.  From the print dialog, select **"Save as PDF"** for a digital file - or send it directly to your printer

![](/media/2499491233/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> **→ _Best for_**_: sharing research with stakeholders, archiving a finished project, or printing for offline use._

## Drag & Drop - Single Snippets to Another App

The fastest way to move individual snippets to another tool.

Open a Topic, grab the snippet you want, and drag and drop it directly into **Google Docs, Notion, Outlook, Gmail**, or any other app that supports text input.

The content lands exactly where you drop it - no copying, no formatting lost.

![](/media/2499494863/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> **→ _Best for_**_: quickly moving one or a few snippets into a document you're already working on._
