There are 2 ways you can add a new topic to Cluing: when browsing through the **extension** or directly through our **Dashboard**.

Topics can be **[[articles/11768652-reordering-topics|sorted into Clusters]]** when first created, but can also be organized later on by just dragging and dropping them into the right container.

## Creating new topic when using the extension

Creating a new Topic right from the extension is super easy! Here’s how:

1.  Open the extension.

2.  Click the **"+"** icon in the top right corner of the extension.

To create a snippet, simply **select any text** on the webpage. A snippet preview will appear in the extension - just **press Enter** or click ‘Create’ to save it.

## Creating new topic on the Dashboard

To create a new topic from the Dashboard, follow these steps:

1.  Find the cluster where you want to create the new topic.

2.  Hover your mouse cursor over the cluster title - you’ll see a **"+"** button appear.

3.  Click on it, and the new topic will be created.

## Topics shared with me

Aside from the topics you create, you can become a contributor on others' topics as well.

If someone shares a Topic with you, it will appear under **“Shared”** in your Dashboard sidebar, listed as a **Shared Topic**.
