When you create a new Topic on your dashboard, you’ll see a **“Summarize Link”** option in the empty state.

![](/media/2405712472/image.webp)

Click on it, paste any webpage link into the field, and hit **Create**.

![](/media/2405713030/image.webp)

Cluing AI will automatically **analyze the page**, **extract the key points**, and turn them into a clean snippet inside your Topic.

This is a quick way to save and understand content from any page without reading everything manually.
