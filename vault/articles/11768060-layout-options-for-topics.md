Cluing offers different ways to view your Topic's content to fit your workflow.

## **Board View (Default)**

This is the default view. It provides a clear, linear flow of your snippets.

![](/media/2511990570/image.webp)

## **Grid View**

Switch to Grid View for a more compact overview of your snippets.

This can be great for quickly scanning through a lot of information or for a different perspective on your research.

![](/media/2511980523/image.webp)

## Open a snippet in Grid View

When you click "**Open"** on a snippet while in Grid View, a side menu appears on the right. There, you can see existing **comments** (_if the snippet has any_) and also add new ones.

![](/media/2511984289/image.webp)

Want to see how it works in action? Check out our **[interactive video tutorial.](https://app.arcade.software/share/zNmFR678aAOLVcHCWwuj) 👈🏼**
