Turn your entire AI conversation into a structured asset.

You can instantly convert any AI Chat session into a workspace snippet, making it easy to save, organize, and reference later.

## How to Extract Your Chat?

1.  Open Chat you want to save

2.  Open [[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|Canvas]]

3.  Click "Save to Topic" - in the top right corner

4.  Choose the Topic you want to save it

The system will process the entire canvas and generate a structured snippet within your workspace.

## View and save

1.  **Locate snippet -** This will open entire canvas in Reader

2.  **Reader -** In Reader, you can see the full text of the extracted canvas

3.  **Save and Share -** From Reader, you can highlight specific parts of the extracted text to create even more granular snippets or simply keep the entire extraction as a primary reference point

It’s the fastest way to save a brainstorm, archive important AI suggestions, and share those insights with your team without scrolling through message history.
