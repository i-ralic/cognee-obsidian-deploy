If the Cluing extension is on in your browser, you can **save snippets** without opening the extension. There is a Highlighter button that pops up exactly where you’re highlighting.

## How it works

When you highlight any text on a webpage, a small menu will appear. There, you will have three options to save snippet:

1.  **Snippet:** Click this to turn your selection into a **snippet**. It opens a quick-save window where you can double-check the details

2.  **[[articles/11783251-bookmarks-save-now-read-later|Bookmark]]:** Use this to save the entire URL to your Topic. It’s perfect for building a "read later" list.

3.  **[[articles/14211688-summarize-webpage|Summarize]]:** This action summarizes the entire page and saves that summary as a snippet.

> [!warning]
> 💡 **Pro Tip:** If you don't see the menu appearing, make sure the **“Hide highlighter button”** option is turned **OFF** in your extension settings.

## **The Quick-Save Window**

After you click one of the buttons, a small window appears so you can stay organized without leaving the page. Here you can:

-   **Pick your spot:** Choose exactly which **Cluster** and **Topic** this research belongs to

-   **Add your thoughts:** Drop in a comment, add a tag, or mention a teammate to start a conversation

-   **Confirm:** One click and you’re done

## **AI Suggestions**

To make things even faster, our "Smart Assistant" looks at your previous research and suggests the best **Topic**, **Title**, and **Tags** for your new snippet. Most of the time, you won’t even have to type just confirm and keep moving.
