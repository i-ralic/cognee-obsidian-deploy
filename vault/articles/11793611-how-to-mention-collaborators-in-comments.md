With comments, you can easily mention users and bring their attention to the conversation.

## How To Mention Your Collaborators

1.  Start typing a comment

2.  In your input field, type the "**@**" followed by the name of the person you want to mention

3.  User names will automatically appear in a **dropdown menu**

4.  When you save your comment, the mentioned user will receive an **email notification** letting them know they've been tagged

> [!danger]
> 📌 If the collaborators have **Editor or Commenter** role on Cluster, they will be able to mention other users within Topics inside that cluster.

## Collaborator Suggestion List

As you type, you'll see a list of your **previously added collaborators** to choose from.

You can **quickly select** the right collaborator from the list and keep moving. This way, you will reduce mistakes like tagging the wrong person or misspelling names.

## Who Can Be Mentioned In Comments?

**[[articles/11793739-mention-external-users-by-email|You can mention anyone]] - just by typing their email address.**

They’ll receive an email notification with the snippet details and a link to locate the original source.

When mentioning someone, you can also assign one of four roles: **Viewer**, **Commenter**, **Editor**, or **Mention Only**.

-   If you choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**, the person will be added to the entire Topic and will have access based on the role you assign.

-   If you choose **Mention Only**, they’ll see **only that specific snippet** - not the rest of the Topic.
