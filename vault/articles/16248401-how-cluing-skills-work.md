Every Skill in Cluing has two layers: the **_instructions_** that tell your agent what to do, and the **_knowledge_** that helps it do the job right.

The instructions live in the **_Skill_**. The knowledge lives in a connected _Topic_.

Together, they give your agent a workflow and the context to run it — every time, without you rewriting a prompt from scratch.

## **1\. Instructions layer**

This defines the workflow your agent should follow.

For example:

-   How to write SEO articles

-   How to analyze customer interviews

-   How to prepare competitor research

-   How to review product feedback

You create these instructions once, and reuse them whenever you need that workflow.

## **2\. Knowledge layer**

The knowledge behind your Skill lives in Topics.

Topics can include:

-   Saved web pages

-   PDF documents

-   LinkedIn, Reddit, and X posts

-   YouTube videos

-   Notes and comments

-   Research insights from your team

This gives your agent the context it needs to produce better results.
