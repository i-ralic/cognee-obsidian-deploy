A snippet in Cluing is a **highlighted piece of text** you save. It's a quick preview of that specific section on a webpage.

![](/media/2004675407/Group-2.webp)

## How to add snippets?

Just highlight any text on a webpage or PDF and hit **Enter** or click the "**Create"** button to save it.
