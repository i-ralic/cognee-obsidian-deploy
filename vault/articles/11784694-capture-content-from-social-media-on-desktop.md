## Save LinkedIn Posts as Snippets

-   ### Open the Extension

To save posts from LinkedIn, all you have to do is go to the LinkedIn app and open the extension in your browser.

-   ### Navigate to the Save Button

You'll find a "**Save**" button located beneath each post in your LinkedIn feed.

Clicking this button automatically captures the post's description and stores it as a snippet within the extension, making it easy to revisit later.

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note:** If you save a LinkedIn highlight from your feed, you won't be able to find the original post later. To easily locate it, **always use the "Save" button**.

> [!warning]
> **💡 _Why save LinkedIn posts in Cluing?_**
> ​
> Cluing is a safe place for your LinkedIn ideas. Even if someone deletes that LinkedIn post, what you save in Cluing stays safe.
>
> Plus, you can easily share these saved posts with your [[articles/11767739-how-to-share-a-topic|collaborators for private chats]] and teamwork. And when you need to find something later, Cluing's search makes it quick and easy.

## Save Reddit Posts and Comments as Snippets

-   ### Open the Extension

To save posts from Reddit, all you have to do is go to Reddit and open the extension in your browser.

-   ### Navigate to the Save Button

You'll find a "**Save**" button located beneath each post on your Reddit home page. Clicking this button automatically captures the post's description and stores it **as a snippet** within the extension, making it easy to revisit later.

-   ### Save Comment from Reddit

You can also find a "**Save**" button located next to each comment on Reddit posts.

Clicking this button automatically captures the comment and stores it **as a snippet** within the extension, making it easy to revisit later.
