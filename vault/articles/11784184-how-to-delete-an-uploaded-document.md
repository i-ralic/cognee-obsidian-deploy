If you no longer need a Document in your workspace, you can remove it in just a few clicks. Here is how:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Click **Reader** in the left sidebar

3.  On the Reader page, click the **Document** icon in the top-left corner

4.  A list of all uploaded documents will appear

5.  Click the **three-dot (⋮) menu** next to the document you want to delete

6.  Select **Delete and confirm**

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note**: If you try to delete a document that has saved snippets, **a warning** will pop up to let you know before you confirm.
