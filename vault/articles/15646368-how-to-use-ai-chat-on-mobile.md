Before you start, make sure to download the Cluing mobile app - available for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

## **How to Open AI Chat**

1.  Open the Cluing app

2.  Tap the **Chat icon** in the bottom navigation bar

![](/media/2500090348/image.webp)

## **What You Can Do**

Once in Chat, you can:

-   **Choose an** **AI model** based on what you need - ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Claude, or DeepSeek, or use **custom-made agents** for specific tasks

-   **Type your prompt** and get instant answers with **source links** for easy verification

-   Use **@** to **mention a specific Topic** from your workspace and give the chat context from your own research

-   **Upload** your own documents or images

-   **Save conversation** as a snippet to keep it alongside the rest of your research

-   Type **"Add research to Canvas and Open Canvas"** to turn your findings into a structured, visual document you can always save as a snippet
    ​

![](/media/2500117263/image.webp)

## **Chat History**

All your past conversations are **saved automatically**. You can search through them, rename chats to stay organized, or delete ones you no longer need.
