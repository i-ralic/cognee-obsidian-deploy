Whether you’re finishing projects or cleaning up unused topics, the Topic Archive helps you organize your content while keeping it easy to access later.

## **How to archive a topic on the Dashboard:**

1.  Go to the topic you want to archive.

2.  Click the menu button for that topic.

3.  Select **Archive** from the menu.

This will automatically move the topic to the **Archive Cluster**, which is located in your side panel.

![](/media/2472546377/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> **📌 Note:** Archiving does not permanently delete your topics. However, if you're sure that you no longer need a topic, you can [[articles/11748718-how-to-delete-a-topic|delete it permanently]] **from the Archive Cluster**.

## **How to archive a topic in the Extension:**

To archive a topic in the extension, simply click the **dropdown menu** and find the topic you want to archive.

You’ll see an archive icon - click it, and your topic will move to the Archive Cluster.
