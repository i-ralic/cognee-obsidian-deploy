When sharing a topic via a **[[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|Clu.ing link]]**, you have the option to **customize its appearance** however you prefer.

You can add your **brand colors**, **logo**, or other visual elements to match your style.

Here’s how to customize your shared topics:

-   Go to your **Dashboard**.

-   Click the **workspace name** in the top left corner.

-   Select **Settings and Members**.

-   There, you’ll find the option to **upload your logo** and **choose your brand color**.

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note:** Only the **workspace owner** can customize the logo and brand color for shared topics.
