In the comment below a snippet, you can mention:

-   **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|Collaborators]]** from your shared workspace

-   **Cluing users** who aren’t in your workspace (but have a Cluing account)

-   **[[articles/11793739-mention-external-users-by-email|External users]]** without a Cluing account

-   **[[articles/11776096-mentioning-users-from-slack|People from Slack]]** (if you’ve connected the Slack app to Cluing)
