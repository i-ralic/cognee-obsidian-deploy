The **Duplicate To** feature lets you reuse existing content without rebuilding it from scratch. You can duplicate an individual snippet or an entire Topic and choose where its copy should be saved.

> [!warning]
> 📌 Duplicating creates a separate copy and changes made to the copy do not change the original content.

## What you can duplicate

You can use **Duplicate To** for:

-   **Snippets** - copy a snippet to another Topic or create another copy in the same Topic

-   **Topics** - copy a complete Topic to another Cluster in the same workspace or to another workspace you can access

## Duplicate a snippet

1.  Find the snippet you want to duplicate

2.  Open the snippet menu (**three dots**)

3.  Select **Duplicate To**

4.  Choose the destination Topic

Comments are included by default. The duplicate also keeps the original author, helping preserve attribution and context.

> [!warning]
> 📌 If you do not want to include its comments, select **Don’t copy comments**.

## Duplicate a Topic

1.  Open the Topic you want to duplicate

2.  Open the Topic **menu**

3.  Select **Duplicate To**

4.  Choose the destination:

    -   Another Cluster in the current workspace

    -   Another workspace you can access.

Cluing creates a separate copy of the Topic and its snippets in the selected destination. You can then rename, reorganize, or edit the copied Topic without changing the original.

> [!warning]
> 📌 Snippet comments are included by default. To exclude comments from duplicating, select **Don’t copy comments**
