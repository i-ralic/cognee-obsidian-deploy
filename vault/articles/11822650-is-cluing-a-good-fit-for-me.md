Most tools help you remember. Cluing helps you actually do something with what you remember.

It's **a** **second brain** that doesn't just store what you find - it **executes on it**. Your saved links, notes, and insights become active context that your AI can pull from, reason over, and turn into real output.

If you're constantly reading, collecting, and then trying to make something out of it all - Cluing was built for you.

It's especially useful for people in:

-   **[[collections/11578905-marketing-and-communications|Content Marketing]]** - research, drafts, and briefs grounded in what you've actually saved

-   **[[collections/13202434-consulting|Consulting]]** - client insights and industry signals, always within reach when you need them

-   **[[collections/13202526-legal-and-compliance|Legal and Compliance]]** - documents, references, and precedents that your AI can work with

-   **Research & Development** - collected knowledge that doesn't just sit there

Whether you're crafting marketing copy, writing reports, building case files, or producing technical content - Cluing turns your knowledge base into a tool that thinks with you.

The best place to start is getting our [browser extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cluing-shareable-notes-on/jjefmmjicedljonbpdoeceklnhobmolo) and [[articles/13833323-cluing-mobile-apps|mobile app]].💛
