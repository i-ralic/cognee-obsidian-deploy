If you want to add external users, you could do so by mentioning them by email. This will send them an email with a snippet of text and instructions to register.

## Here's how:

1.  Under the snippet, start typing your comment (either in the browser **Extension** or on the **Dashboard**).

2.  In the input field, type **"@"** followed by the email address of the person you want to mention

3.  Choose the **role** you want to assign to that person

4.  Save your comment

The person receives an **email** letting them know they've been mentioned, along with a snippet of context and instructions to register

## Collaborator roles

When you mention someone, you can assign one of the following roles:

-   **Viewer** — can only view the content.

-   **Commenter** — can view and leave comments.

-   **Editor** — can view, comment, and edit the content.

-   **[[articles/11830845-mention-only|Only Mention]]** — lets you mention a person **without sharing the entire Topic** with them. They receive an email notification with a snippet of details and an option to locate it.
