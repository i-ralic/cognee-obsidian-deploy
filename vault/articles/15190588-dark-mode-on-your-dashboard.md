> [[collections/13994794-workspace-and-account-settings|Workspace and Account Settings]]

To change your theme, go to your dashboard and click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then open **Appearance**.

From there, you can switch between Light and Dark mode with a single click.

<video src="/media/2405584518/inline-23c7936c6b9086faa97fb84334a49290.mp4" aria-label="DarkMode.gif-4894" width="1908" height="906" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Dark theme is easier on the eyes, especially in low-light environments, and can help reduce eye strain during long sessions.

> [!note]
> 📌 Dark Mode for the Cluing Extension is separate from your Dashboard settings, so it won’t switch automatically. **[[articles/15190722-dark-mode-in-the-extension|Here's how to change it]] 👈🏼**
