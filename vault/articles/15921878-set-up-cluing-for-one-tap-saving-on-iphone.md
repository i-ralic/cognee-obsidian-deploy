## How to add Cluing to your iPhone Share Menu Favorites:

1.  Open the webpage, LinkedIn post, YouTube video, or any content you want to save to Cluing.

2.  Tap the **Share** button.

3.  If Cluing is not visible in your app options, swipe left on the apps and tap **More**.

4.  Tap **Edit** in the top-right corner.

5.  Find **Cluing** and tap the **+** icon next to it.

6.  Cluing will now appear in your Favorites, making it easier to save content the next time you use the Share Menu.
