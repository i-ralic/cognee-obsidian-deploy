Content is king but creating high-quality, engaging content _consistently_ is the hard part. Juggling research, collaborating with teammates, and keeping a cohesive message across every platform can feel like a full-time job on top of your actual job.

Cluing brings your **whole content workflow into one place** - from research to drafting to repurposing - so your team spends less time hunting for information and more time creating content that actually resonates.

### **The Content Creation Struggle:**

Traditional content workflows are scattered by design: research lives in a maze of bookmarks and browser tabs, feedback happens across endless email and Slack threads, and revisions get lost between document versions.

-   **Researchers** struggle to organize their findings.

-   **Writers** hit a wall trying to synthesize it all into something coherent.

-   **Editors** waste time chasing down the latest draft and untangling feedback.

The result? Wasted hours, inconsistent messaging, and content that doesn't hit as hard as it should.

## Cluing: Your Content Marketing Powerhouse

Cluing keeps every stage of your content workflow under one roof. Here's how each piece fits together.

### 📌 Streamlined Research

Say goodbye to messy bookmarks and overflowing folders. With the **[[articles/11839482-how-to-install-the-cluing-extension-and-create-your-first-topic|Cluing browser extension]]**, you can highlight text on any webpage and save it straight into a Topic.

A small **[[articles/11928105-highlighter-button|Highlighter]]** menu pops up right where you select, giving you three options:

-   **Snippet** - turn your selection into a saved snippet

-   **Bookmark** - save the whole page for a "read later" list

-   **Summarize** - let AI condense the entire page into a snippet in seconds

Researching a competitor's blog post? Highlight the impactful stats, and they're all filed in one place, ready to reuse.

You can capture from **PDFs** the same way, and the **[[articles/12267724-cluing-reader|Cluing Reader]]** gives you a clean, distraction-free view to read and highlight - while preserving the original source even if the page later changes.

### 📩 Turn Newsletters Into Research on Autopilot

Content marketers live in industry newsletters but those insights usually vanish into a crowded inbox. Cluing fixes that.

Give any Topic its own **dedicated email address**, then **[[articles/14058819-organize-your-newsletters-with-cluing|subscribe to your favorite]] newsletters with it**. Every new issue skips your inbox and lands directly in that Topic as an organized snippet, ready to highlight and reuse.

Already subscribed with your personal email? You can also **[[articles/14058347-forward-emails-into-your-knowledge-base|forward]]** individual issues to the Topic address. Either way, your "Industry Trends" or "Competitor Watch" Topic quietly fills itself with fresh, on-theme research every week.

> [!warning]
> 📌 Email addresses depend on your plan: **Free** - 1 active address; **Personal Pro & Team** - multiple addresses for different Topics.

### 💡 Capture Your Own Ideas Too

Research is only half the story - the best content often starts with _your_ original angle.

**[[articles/13169408-how-to-add-ideas-as-snippets|Idea snippets]]** (marked with a 💡 icon) let you jot down a headline, a hook, or a campaign concept the moment it strikes, and store it right alongside your collected research. Add a title, a **#tag**, or link it to a source, and your raw thinking lives in the same Topic as everything that will support it.

![](/media/2541422040/image.webp)

### 📌 Effortless Collaboration

**Share any Topic** with your team via a **clu.ing link** or by inviting people by email, and assign each person a role - **Viewer, Commenter, or Editor**.

From there, teammates can **highlight sections, add comments, apply #tags, and @mention** each other directly on the research.

No more juggling five versions of the same doc or losing feedback in a Slack scroll - everyone works on the same source of truth, in context.

### 📌 From Research to Draft with AI Chat & Canvas

This is where Cluing really pulls ahead. Once your research lives in a Topic, you can **ground AI Chat in your own content** instead of the open web.

Open **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|Chat]]**, type **@**, and select your Topic and Cluing feeds everything saved inside it to the AI as context.

Try prompts like:

-   _"Analyze my @Sustainable Living topic and draft 3 blog post outlines."_

-   _"Write 10 social hooks from the stats saved in @Q3 Campaign."_

When you're ready to build a real document, just say **"Add research to Canvas and Open Canvas"** - AI Canvas structures your research into a clean, editable draft you can refine right there, then **save back into a Topic as a snippet**.

### 📌 Enhanced Content Quality

By centralizing research, streamlining feedback, and grounding your AI in _your_ material, better content follows naturally.

Writers have every **relevant insight at their fingertips**, editors give clear feedback in context, and the whole team keeps a consistent brand voice.

### 📌 Content Repurposing Made Easy

Cluing's organized structure makes repurposing effortless. Pull key highlights from existing content to spin up social posts, infographics, or **email newsletters**, and let AI Chat do the heavy lifting.

One blog post can become a week of social snippets, all sourced from content you've already saved.

### Real-World Example 🌱

A content marketing team is preparing a blog series on sustainable living. Instead of juggling bookmarks, documents, and chat threads, they manage the entire project inside Cluing.

-   **Research** - The team saves articles, reports, videos, and newsletters into a shared **Sustainable Living** Topic. They highlight important statistics, use **Summarize** for longer articles, and capture their own ideas as 💡 Idea snippets whenever inspiration strikes.

-   **Plan** - Once enough research has been collected, they open AI Chat, mention **@Sustainable Living**, and ask Cluing to identify recurring themes, recommend article ideas, and generate outlines for the series. The team decides who will write each piece.

-   **Write** - Writers use the Topic as their single source of truth. They ask AI to turn the research into a first draft in Canvas, refine it, and save important sections back into the Topic as snippets. Throughout the process, teammates leave comments, apply **#tags**, and **@mention** each other for feedback.

-   **Review** - Editors review the draft alongside the original research, making sure every claim is backed by a source and that the messaging stays consistent across the series.

-   **Repurpose** - After publishing, the same Topic becomes the foundation for repurposing. The team asks **AI Chat to generate** LinkedIn posts, newsletter blurbs, social captions, and email copy - all grounded in the research they've already collected.
