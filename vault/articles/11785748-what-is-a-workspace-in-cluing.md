> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/13994794-workspace-and-account-settings|Workspace and Account Settings]]

A Workspace in Cluing is your central place for collecting, organizing, and collaborating on content from across the web. It keeps your highlights, comments, and clusters together in one secure, easy-to-manage space.

Each user has **a** **Personal Workspace** created automatically after registering, but can also be a part of **a Team Workspace** as well.

If you're using the **Team Version**, you can:

-   Be part of multiple team workspaces

-   [[articles/11791174-how-to-switch-between-workspaces|Switch between them]] from the top left panel

-   Invite teammates and manage access

-   Customize the [[articles/11785971-how-to-rename-the-workspace|workspace name]]

Only **workspace owners** can access settings and invite members.

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Important note** - [[articles/11791995-team-version|Upgrading to a Team plan]] after using a personal workspace creates a new workspace where team members can be added. It will not automatically transfer all the snippets and topics you created in your Personal Workspace.
