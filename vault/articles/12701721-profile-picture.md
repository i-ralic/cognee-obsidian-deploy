You can personalize your Cluing account by uploading a profile picture. This small detail helps make your collaborations friendlier and makes it easier to identify your teammates.

## How to add a Profile Picture

Steps:

1.  Navigate to Settings in the upper-right corner

2.  Open User settings

3.  Spot the "User Info" section

    1.  Click on the avatar icon

    2.  Choose the picture you want to upload

    3.  Adjust it as you like

    4.  Click the **Upload button**

## **Where will your Profile picture appear**

Your chosen picture will be visible in key collaborative areas within Cluing:

-   **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|Teammate Mentions:]]** When you use the mention(_@Name_) to tag a teammate, their profile picture will be displayed next to their name.

-   **Share Panel:** Your picture (_and the pictures of your team_) will appear in the share panel, providing clear visual recognition of who has access to a Topic or Cluster.

-   **[[articles/11822856-what-s-a-clu-ing-link|Clu.ing Link:]]** On public Topics, you can see you Profile picture in top right corner
