Cluing makes the research process easier by letting you quickly highlight and organize important info from webpages, PDFs, YouTube videos, and social media like X, LinkedIn, and Reddit.

The key benefits are:

1.  Everything you save is **collaborative** and **locatable -** comments can be attached directly to key knowledge, and looking it up again takes seconds - the locate button will take you directly to the segment of a YouTube video or the chosen paragraph (or anything else you saved).

2.  The **data is saved "as is"** - even if the original data is changed or completely wiped from the internet, your Cluing snippets are always there for reference, along with a time stamp, the original link, and the person who found it.

3.  All insights become **searchable** - unlike pasting a link to Slack or a spreadsheet, Cluing is easy to search, and the data that was saved can be reused time after time.
