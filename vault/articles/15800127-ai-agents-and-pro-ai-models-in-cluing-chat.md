Cluing Chat gives you two ways to work with AI:

-   **Custom AI Agents** - specialized assistants designed for specific tasks.

-   **Pro AI Models** - access to the latest flagship AI models from leading providers.

## Custom AI Agents

Custom AI Agents are built to help you complete specific types of work faster and with better results. Instead of starting from scratch, you can choose an agent that's already optimized for your task.

Available agents include:

-   **😊 Intern:** A general-purpose assistant for everyday workspace tasks, quick questions, summaries, drafts, and guidance when you do not need a specialized agent.

-   **🧐 Researcher**: Designed for research tasks. Gather information, analyze sources, summarize findings, and explore topics more efficiently.

-   ⚖️ **Legal Consultant:** Provides professional legal analysis and guidance to help you better understand legal documents, contracts, regulations, and compliance-related questions.

-   **✍🏼 Creative Writer:** Helps you write and refine content in a natural, engaging way. Use it for brainstorming ideas, drafting articles, improving tone, or turning rough thoughts into clear, well-written text.

-   **📰 Press Release Writer:** Create professional press releases from scratch or review and improve existing ones before publication with a polished, media-ready tone.

-   📢 **Marketing Expert:** Get help with marketing strategies, campaign planning, messaging, positioning, content ideas, and go-to-market plans.

-   **💻 Code Reviewer**: Review code, identify potential issues, suggest improvements, and explain best practices.

-   **📊 Data Analyst:** Interpret data, identify trends, summarize findings, and help turn raw information into actionable insights.

-   **📂 [[articles/15191356-self-organizing-workspace-through-ai-chat|Workspace Organizer]]:** Organize your Cluing workspace by structuring Topics, improving organization, and keeping your knowledge base tidy.

## Pro AI Models

[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|Cluing Chat]] also gives you access to the **latest AI models** from leading AI providers, allowing you to choose the model that best fits your task.

Current Pro AI providers include:

-   **OpenAI**

-   **Anthropic**

-   **Grok**

-   **DeepSeek**

## [[articles/15551899-cluing-ai-chat-models-vs-chat-agents|Which should you use?]]

-   Choose a **Custom AI Agent** when you want an assistant that's already optimized for a specific type of work.

-   Choose a **Pro AI Model** when you want to work directly with a particular AI model or compare responses across different providers.

You can **switch between agents** and models at any time during your conversation.
