Ever worried about losing your highlighted snippets if a webpage disappears or changes? 🤔

With the **Cluing Reader**, you can now save, highlight, and collaborate safely, without losing context. It even [[articles/13390541-how-to-add-webpage-annotations-on-mobile#h_fc5e1cc6b2|works on mobile]].

<video src="/media/2302372771/readerColorHighlight.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1908" height="908" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Here’s how you can use it to make your research safer, cleaner, and more collaborative.

## Two Ways to Open the Reader

### 1) From Your Dashboard

-   Go to your **Cluing dashboard**.

-   In the **left sidebar**, click on "**Reader"**.

-   **Paste the webpage link** you want to open.

-   You can now **highlight, comment, tag, and collaborate** as usual.

![](/media/2455240725/image.webp)

![](/media/2467934321/image-31.webp)

### 2) From a Snippet

-   Click on the **menu options (three dots)** on any snippet.

-   Select **"Open in Reader"**.

-   The webpage opens in the Reader, and you can continue to **highlight, comment, tag, and collaborate** as usual.

> [!warning]
> **💡 Why Use Cluing Reader?**
>
> -   All your snippets are **always locatable and safe**, even if the original page disappears.
>
> -   You can **collaborate with your team** in real-time, adding context through comments and tags.
>
> -   Works **even on devices without the browser extension**.
