When someone shares a certain Topic or Cluster with you, it will appear in the **“Shared”** section in both the extension and the Dashboard.

If the link-sharing settings change (or your access is revoked by the owner of the topic), you'll no longer have the option to access any of the data.

## Where to Find Topics Shared With You?

## On the Dashboard

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**.

2.  In the left sidebar with all your topics and clusters, scroll all the way down.

3.  If someone has shared a topic or cluster with you, you’ll see it listed under the **Shared** cluster.

![](/media/2497114453/image.webp)

##
In the Extension

1.  Open the **extension**.

2.  Click to open the **drop-down menu** with your topics and clusters.

3.  Scroll all the way down.

4.  If someone has shared a topic or cluster with you, it will appear **at the bottom** of the extension.

> [!warning]
> 📍 Your access depends on the **[[articles/11792004-permission-levels-explained|permission level]]** assigned by the Topic Owner. If you have a Viewer or Commenter role, you won't be able to add snippets to the topic. **To add snippets, you need an Editor role**.
