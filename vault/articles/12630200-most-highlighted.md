Turn on this preference to see the sections most highlighted by other Cluing users. It gives you an **instant overview** of the key content, helping you focus on the most valuable information.

## **How to activate it**

1.  Go to the webpage

2.  Open the Cluing Extension

3.  Click on **Settings in the top right corner**

4.  In the **Preference menu**, activate **Most Highlighted**
