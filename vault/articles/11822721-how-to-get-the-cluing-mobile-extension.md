Key insights can happen at any time, so we do our best to be available on the go, too.

> [!warning]
> Mobile extensions are only available on **Edge browser**

## Mobile extension for Android

There are only 3 steps to start:

1.  **Open the Edge menu**: Click the button in the bottom right corner to open the menu.

2.  **Open the extensions list**: In the menu, find the **Extensions** option.

3.  **Scroll down to** Cluing: Scroll through the list of extensions until you find Cluing.

![](/media/2009070558/Edge-Extension.webp)

## **Mobile extension for IOS**

1.  Open the **Edge browser** on your phone.

2.  Tap the **three lines (_menu options_)** in the bottom right corner.

3.  Select **Extensions**.

4.  Find Cluing in the list and tap **Get**.

![](/media/2009071437/Edge-Ext-2.webp)

##
Installing the extension on Firefox

To get the extension, visit [the Cluing page on the Add-ons store](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/android/addon/collabwriting-shareable-notes/) from your Firefox browser.

Click "Install", log in, and you're ready to go 🚀

## What can you do on mobile?

With the Cluing mobile extension, you can:

-   Highlight key information from articles and reports (PDFs included)

-   Add comments and insights directly to saved snippets

-   Keep track of sources and organize research into structured knowledge

-   Share topics and clusters with others
