## **How to Leave a Shared Cluster**

You can leave a cluster shared with you from your **Dashboard**. Here’s how:

-   Go to your **Dashboard**.

-   In the left sidebar with all your topics and clusters, **scroll all the way down**.

-   Under the **Shared** cluster, you’ll see topics and clusters shared with you.

-   Hover your cursor over the cluster name, and the **Leave** option will appear.

-   Click ''**Leave''** to exit the cluster.

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note:** Once you leave a cluster, this action **cannot be undone**. To regain access, you'll need to request it again from the cluster owner.
