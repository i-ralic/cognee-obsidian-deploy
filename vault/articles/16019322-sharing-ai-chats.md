You can share any AI Chat conversation with with anyone who has a Cluing account. Teammates get a read-only view of the conversation, and can fork it to continue on their own - without changing your original.

## **How to share a conversation**

1.  Open AI Chat History

2.  Click the 3 dots next to the conversations

3.  Click share button

4.  When the Share panel opens, set **General Access** to the level you want

5.  Copy the share link and send it to the people

## **General Access options**

When sharing, you can choose one of three access levels:

-   **Restricted:** No one else can see the conversation - only you have access

-   **Workspace:** Only members of your workspace can see the conversation

-   **Public:** Anyone with a Cluing account and the link can see the conversation

> [!warning]
> 📌 To stop sharing at any time, reopen the Share panel and set **General Access** back to **Restricted**.

## **What people you share with can do**

-   **View:** They can read the full conversation but can't edit it or send new messages

-   **Duplicate:** They can click **Duplicate** to create their own copy and continue the discussion independently - your original conversation stays untouched

> [!warning]
> 📌 Only owner can share, unshare, or edit the original conversation
