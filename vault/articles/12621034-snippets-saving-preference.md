You can now choose whether your saved snippets appear at the **top** or **bottom** of the list in the extension. Here’s how to change this preference:

## How to change snippet order

**Open the Extension**
Launch the Cluing extension in your browser.

**Go to Settings**
Click on the **Settings** icon located in the upper-right corner of the extension.
​

![](/media/1997690436/OfcZbr1srn.webp)

**Choose Snippet Placement**

Look for the option **"Add Cards to the Bottom"** and toggle it on or off depending on your preference.

![](/media/2219305012/CardsToTheBottom.webp)

Once done, all your new snippets will be saved according to your chosen preference-either at the top or at the bottom of your list. 🙌🏼
