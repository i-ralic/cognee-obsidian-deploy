Cluing is designed to make sharing your ideas and research easy.

Whether you're working with a team or want to share your findings with a wider audience, here's how to get started:

## Sharing Options

Cluing offers flexible sharing options to control who sees your work.

You can share:

-   **Individual Topics**

-   **Entire Clusters**

-   **Entire Workspace** \- reserved for Team Version

## Two ways to share

Cluing offers two primary ways to share your research and ideas:

1.  **[[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|Sharing with a link]]** - Generate a shareable link for broader access

2.  **[[articles/11792225-add-people-to-a-topic-via-email|Adding users directly via email]] -** Invite specific people for more controlled collaboration

## Permission levels

Cluing's permission system is designed to give you **granular control** over who can access and interact with your work.

Here's a breakdown:

1.  **Viewer -** Can only view the content

2.  **Commenter -** Can view and add comments

3.  **Editor -** Can view, comment, and edit the content

4.  **Owner -** Has full control, including sharing and managing permissions

> [!danger]
> **📌 Note:** Team workspace members have a default EDITOR role on each Cluster/Topic inside of it unless the owner of the Cluster sets it to be Restricted.
