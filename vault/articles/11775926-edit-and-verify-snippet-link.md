Sometimes webpages change, and your snippet links may need updating. If the link in your snippet is **no longer valid**, here’s a quick way to update it and keep everything accurate:

1.  Find a snippet with the link you want to edit

2.  Open snippet menu

3.  Click on "**Edit Link**"

4.  Edit the link from the snippet header

5.  Click save

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note:** When choosing to update all snippets with the same link, double-check that this is your intention, as it **will affect multiple snippets**.
