> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/14602689-cluing-on-the-go|Cluing On The Go]]

**Cluing's mobile app** lets you capture, organize, and work with your research wherever you are. Whether you're reading an article, watching a video, or chatting with AI - you don't need to be at your desk to get things done.

## **[[articles/15645745-how-to-save-content-as-a-snippet-while-browsing-on-mobile|Capture Online Content]]**

Save anything you come across while browsing - webpages, YouTube videos, LinkedIn posts, X, TikTok, and more - by tapping the **Share button** in your browser or app and selecting Cluing.

> [!warning]
> 📍 You can also highlight and annotate [[articles/13390541-how-to-add-webpage-annotations-on-mobile|webpages]] directly inside the **built-in Reader**. The same goes for [[articles/12460051-how-to-add-pdf-annotations-on-mobile|PDFs]] - open or upload one and start annotating right away.

## [[articles/15646141-idea-snippets-take-notes-on-mobile|Take Notes]]

Not everything worth saving comes from the internet.

With **Idea Snippets**, you can capture your own thoughts, notes, or something interesting you heard at an event or in a conversation - directly in the app, marked with a 💡 icon to keep them separate from your web research.

## **[[articles/15887367-summarize-any-link|Summarize Any Link]]**

Turn any link into a **ready-to-use snippet** in seconds. Just click the + button in any Topic, paste a link, and Cluing will save a **summarized snippet** for you.

## **Organize and Review**

Browse your Topics and Clusters, **search** through your saved snippets, and **add comments** and tags - all from the app.

## **Collaborate**

**[[articles/15646960-how-to-share-a-topic-on-mobile|Share Topics]]** with teammates and **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|mention collaborators]]** directly in comments, just like on desktop.

## **[[articles/15646368-how-to-use-ai-chat-on-mobile|AI Chat]]**

Use AI Chat to ask questions, get answers with source links, and explore your saved knowledge - all without leaving the app.

Access it by tapping the Chat icon in **the bottom navigation bar**.

You can switch between AI agents (ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek), **mention specific Topics** with '**@**' for added context, and turn your findings into a visual document with Canvas.

## **Browser Extensions**

Prefer working inside your mobile browser? Cluing is available as an extension for **[[articles/11822721-how-to-get-the-cluing-mobile-extension|Edge Mobile]]** [[articles/11822721-how-to-get-the-cluing-mobile-extension|and **Firefox Mobile**]], so you can highlight and save directly while browsing.
