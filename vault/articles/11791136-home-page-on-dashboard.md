When you open the app, the Home Page greets you with a dedicated space to jump right back into your research.

Here’s what you’ll find:

-   **Your Recent Topics** - quickly access the last 4 topics you’ve been working on.

-   **Examples & Community Topics** - explore examples and contributions from our team and community to spark new ideas.

Just log in and continue where you left off - or get inspired by fresh insights! 🏠

**To return to the Home Page** from any topic, simply click on the **Home Page Icon** in the Topic Navigation Bar.
