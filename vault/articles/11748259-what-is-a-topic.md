**Think of a Topic as a folder for a specific subject or project.** It's where you collect everything related to that topic in one organized place.

**A single Topic can include content from multiple sources** - Reddit, LinkedIn, X, webpages, PDFs, Kindle, YouTube, newsletters, and more- and can have **multiple contributors**, making it easy to build a shared knowledge base.

For larger projects, you can organize multiple Topics into [[articles/11768730-what-is-a-cluster|Clusters]] to keep related work structured and easy to navigate.

![](/media/2513833912/image.webp)

## Topics help you:

-   **Organize your research** - Keep everything related to a subject in one place, regardless of where it came from

-   **Stay focused** - Break down big projects into manageable areas

-   **Connect insights** - Bring together ideas from different sources and see how they relate

-   **[[articles/11792079-sharing-options|Collaborate]] smoothly** - Work with teammates, share findings, and build knowledge together

> [!warning]
> _In short, a Topic is your central place for collecting, organizing, and working with knowledge from anywhere on the web._
