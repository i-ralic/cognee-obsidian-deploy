With Cluing, you can capture posts and Reels from both Instagram and Facebook and turn them into organized snippets inside your workspace. Even if the original is later deleted, what you save in Cluing stays safe.
​
The flow works the same way as saving **[[articles/15654106-save-tiktok-videos-to-cluing-on-mobile|TikTok videos]]** - if you've done that before, this will feel instantly familiar.

## Save on Mobile

1.  Install the **[[articles/13833323-cluing-mobile-apps|Cluing mobile app]]** (iOS and Android)

2.  Open the Instagram or Facebook post and tap the **Share** button

3.  Select **Cluing** from the list of apps

4.  Cluing generates a snippet - choose a Topic, edit the title, add a comment, and tap **Create**
