**Reactions** are a quick **thumbs-up** way to let snippet creators know you found their work helpful or interesting. It’s a quick way to show appreciation without having to leave a comment.

## How to react to a Snippet?

1.  Find a snippet you like.

2.  Look for the thumbs-up icon inside the snippet.

3.  Click the **thumbs-up 👍🏼** to react

The snippet will show how many likes it has (_including yours_).

## **Who can react to a Snippet?**

Reactions are available only to users with **Editor** or **Commenter** roles. If you're a **Viewer** on a topic, you won’t be able to react, but you’ll still see the total number of likes on the snippet.

## **Who sees your reaction?**

Everyone who views the snippet can see the total number of likes, including yours. 😎
