Legal and compliance teams constantly monitor new regulations, interpret complex requirements, and advise stakeholders on what changes mean in practice.

The challenge isn't only finding updates. It's **preserving the context** behind them:

-   What changed?

-   Why does it matter?

-   Who does it affect?

-   Have we handled a similar situation before?

-   What decisions did we make last time?

Regulatory information is scattered across government websites, legal publications, **PDFs**, internal documents, emails, and conversations. Without **a central system**, valuable knowledge gets lost and teams repeat the same research.

**Cluing** helps compliance teams **capture**, **organize**, and **reuse** regulatory knowledge - with every insight connected to its original source and **enriched with team context**.

## Capture regulatory insights from anywhere

Stay on top of changing requirements without losing important details. With Cluing, you can save insights from:

-   Government and regulatory websites

-   Legal publications

-   [[articles/15442545-document-management-in-reader|PDFs]] and reports

-   Internal documents

-   [[articles/14354712-custom-domain-and-email-address-setup-pro-team|Emails]]

-   Webinars and videos

**Highlight** the exact section that matters, add **comments** explaining its impact, and save it as a Snippet **connected to the original source**.

![](/media/2538028263/image.webp)

Instead of copying regulations into spreadsheets or notes, your team builds a **searchable library** of regulatory knowledge.

## Organize compliance research around your work

Create a structure that matches how your team operates.

**[[articles/11748259-what-is-a-topic|Topics]]**

-   Create dedicated Topics for specific regulations, clients, industries, or compliance areas.

    -   Examples:

        -   GDPR Updates

        -   Financial Regulations

        -   AML Requirements

        -   Data Privacy Reviews

        -   Vendor Compliance

Save relevant **Snippets**, add **comments**, invite **collaborators**, and keep all related research together.

**[[articles/11768730-what-is-a-cluster|Clusters]]**

-   Group related Topics into larger knowledge areas.

    -   For example: **Financial Compliance Cluster**

        -   AML Regulations

        -   Banking Requirements

        -   Risk Assessments

        -   Internal Controls

[[articles/14020043-cluster-visibility-team-workspace|Assign permissions]] at the Cluster level to control who can access sensitive information.

**[[articles/11785748-what-is-a-workspace-in-cluing|Workspace]]**

-   Bring your entire compliance knowledge base together in one shared environment where teams can collaborate securely.

## Analyze regulations with AI using your team's context

Reading every regulatory document from start to finish is not always the best first step.

**[[articles/15442354-upload-documents-to-ai-chat|Upload regulations]]**, policy documents, or compliance reports directly into **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]** and ask Cluing to help identify what matters.

Ask questions like:

-   "Summarize the key changes in this regulation."

-   "What obligations does this introduce for financial institutions?"

-   "Which internal policies should we update?"

-   "Compare this regulation with our previous compliance reviews."

You can also mention existing Topics using **[[articles/15444365-mention-a-topic-in-ai-chat|@TopicName]]** to give AI access to your team's previous research, comments, and decisions.

For example: _"Compare this GDPR update with our previous findings in @DataPrivacyReviews and highlight what changed."_

Because **Cluing AI** **understands your saved context**, responses are based not only on the document itself, but also on your organization's accumulated knowledge.

## Turn regulatory research into actionable reports

Compliance teams often need to transform research into clear updates for executives, clients, or internal teams.

With **[AI Chat and Canvas](https://www.cluing.io/canvas/)**, you can create:

-   Regulatory impact summaries

-   Compliance briefings

-   Audit preparation documents

-   Client update reports

-   Internal policy recommendations

Ask Cluing to draft a summary based on your saved research, refine it in Canvas, and export it when ready.

> [!note]
> **💡 Tip:** For US-based compliance work, [Cluing's Caselaw Search](https://www.cluing.io/caselaw) gives you access to over **6.9 million federal and state cases**. Search, find precedents, and save relevant rulings as snippets to keep your compliance research in one place.

## Collaborate with the right people

Bring legal teams, compliance officers, consultants, and business stakeholders into the exact insights that require their input.

With Cluing, you can:

-   **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|@mention]]** teammates directly on Snippets

-   Discuss regulatory changes through comments

-   Share selected research with **[[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|clu.ing links]]**

-   Assign View, Comment, or Edit **[[articles/11792004-permission-levels-explained|permissions]]**

-   Receive notifications through **[[articles/11776096-mentioning-users-from-slack|Slack]]** or email

Everyone sees the same source, the same discussion, and the reasoning behind each decision.

## Build a regulatory knowledge base that gets smarter over time

Every regulatory review creates valuable organizational knowledge.

Cluing preserves not only the regulation itself, but also:

-   why it mattered

-   how your team interpreted it

-   what actions were taken

-   how similar situations were handled before

Over time, your workspace becomes a searchable compliance knowledge base that helps teams respond faster, prepare better audits, and make decisions with confidence.
