When using AI Chat in Cluing, you can mention any of your Topics directly in your prompt. Simply type **@** and select a Topic from the list.

## How to mention a Topic in AI Chat

1.  Open **AI Chat** from your dashboard.

2.  Type **@** in the prompt field.

3.  Select the Topic you want to use.

4.  Enter your question or request.

5.  Send the prompt.

The AI will use the content from the selected Topic as context and generate a more relevant, personalized response.

For example:

_Analyze my topic **@Short-form videos** and write me 10 viral hooks I can use for my next video._

![](/media/2467465609/image.webp)

When you mention a Topic, Cluing automatically searches through all the content saved inside it and provides that information **as context to the AI**. This allows the AI to generate responses that are grounded in your own research, notes, and collected insights.

## What can you use this for?

**Analyze collected information**

-   Ask AI to identify trends, recurring themes, opportunities, or gaps across the content you've saved.

**Summarize a Topic**

-   Get a concise overview of the most important ideas, findings, or discussions within a Topic.

**Discover patterns**

-   Find common frameworks, strategies, opinions, or insights that appear across multiple sources.

**Answer research questions**

-   Use your saved content as a knowledge base and ask specific questions about the information you've collected.
