If you have connected your Slack workspace with Cluing, you'll be able to directly mention your Slack teammates.

Here's how:

1.  Below the **snippet** you want to share with someone from Slack, type **@** and you’ll see a list of all people from Slack.

2.  Select the person from the list, write your comment, and save it.

3.  The person will immediately get a **Slack notification** and can either **locate the content** or **open the topic**
