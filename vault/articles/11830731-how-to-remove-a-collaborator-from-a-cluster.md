If you want to remove a collaborator you previously added to a cluster, you can do it from the Dashboard.

Here’s how:

1.  Find the cluster from which you want to remove the user.

2.  Click the **⚙️ settings button** next to the cluster name.

3.  A side panel will open on the right showing a list of collaborators.

4.  Find the user you want to remove, click on their current permission, and select **Remove access** from the dropdown menu.

![](/media/1634388578/image.webp)

Once removed, they will no longer have access to that cluster or any of the topics inside it - unless they’ve also been added to a specific topic separately.
