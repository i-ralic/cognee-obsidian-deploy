General Access in Cluing allows you to share a link without directly adding people to a Topic.

If you choose Anyone with the link, you can assign a permission level:

-   **Viewer**

-   **Commenter**

-   **Editor**

Once you set the permission and share the link, anyone who receives it will be able to view, comment, or edit your work - depending on the level you assigned.

![](/media/2499515823/image.webp)

> [!danger]
> 📌 **NOTE**: If the person doesn’t have a Cluing account or isn't logged in, they’ll only be able to view your shared snippets and locate them in context, regardless of the [[articles/11792004-permission-levels-explained|permission level]] you set.

## How to share clu.ing links

### **From the Extension**

-   Select the **Topic** you want to share.

-   Click the **Share** button at the bottom of the extension.

-   Under **General Access**, choose **Anyone with the link**.

-   Set the permission level: **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.

Now, simply **copy the link** at the bottom of the extension and share it with anyone.

### From the Dashboard

-   Go to your **Dashboard**.

-   Open the **Topic** you want to share.

-   Click the **Share** button in the top right corner.

-   Under **General Access**, select **Anyone with the link**.

-   Set the permission level: **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.

> [!danger]
> 📌 If the person has a Cluing account and is **logged in**, the topic you shared via a **clu.ing link** will appear in their **“Shared with me”** cluster inside their workspace.
>
> If they’re **not logged in**, they’ll see a **Login** button in the top right corner when they open the clu.ing link.
