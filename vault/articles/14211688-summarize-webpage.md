Don’t let long articles or deep-dive research slow you down. Whether you’re short on time or just want to see if a page is worth a full read, our **Summarize** feature gives you the "TL;DR" in seconds.

## **How it works**

Whenever you encounter a page you want to digest quickly, look for the **Summarize** option. Our AI will scan the entire page, pick out the most important points, and automatically create a new snippet in your Topic.

## Summarize button

We’ve placed the summarize button into few key spots so it’s always right where you need it, whether you’re deep in a browsing session or just starting a new project on your dashboard.

### Extension

If the extension is open while you’re reading, look at the **bottom right corner**.

![](/media/2204559038/image.webp)

### Highlighter

You don't even need to open the extension to get a summary. Simply **highlight any piece of text** on a webpage, and our Highlighter menu will pop up. The summarize button is one of the **three** that appears

![](/media/2204577478/image.webp)

### Empty Topic

On an **empty Topic**, you'll see a summarize button right in the **middle of your screen**. Just paste a link of any article or webpage, and we’ll instantly turn it into a summarized snippet for you.

![](/media/2204592344/image.webp)
