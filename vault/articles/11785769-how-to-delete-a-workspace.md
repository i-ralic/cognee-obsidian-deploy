To access settings, open the **workspace panel** in the top left corner and click **“Settings and members.”**

<video src="/media/2519095371/delete-workspace.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="986" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note:** Only the workspace **owner** can access these settings.

From here, you can manage your workspace or delete it - but be careful, deletion is **permanent** and cannot be undone.

## Once a workspace is deleted:

-   All content within the workspace will be permanently deleted

-   The deletion applies to all members of the workspace

-   After deletion, you will be redirected to another workspace you are part of
