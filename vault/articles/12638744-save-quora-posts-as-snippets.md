**Open the Extension**

-   To save posts from Quora, all you have to do is go to Quora and **open the extension** in your browser.

**Navigate to the Save Button**

-   You'll find a "**Save**" button located beneath each post on your Quora home page.

-   Clicking this button automatically captures the post's description and stores it **as a snippet** within the application, making it easy to revisit later.
