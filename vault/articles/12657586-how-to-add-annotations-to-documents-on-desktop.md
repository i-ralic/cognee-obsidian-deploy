Anything you highlight in a Document becomes a snippet - saved into a Topic, searchable, and still linked back to the page it came from.

## Before you start

Your Document needs to be open in Reader. Two ways to get it there:

-   **It's on your computer** → [[articles/11784127-how-to-upload-pdfs-to-your-workspace|upload it]]

-   **It's online** **PDF** → [[articles/11784000-how-to-open-an-online-pdf-in-cluing|open it straight from its URL]], or click the Cluing extension icon while viewing it. No need to download it first.

## Highlight and save a snippet

It works exactly like highlighting on any webpage. When your Document opens in Reader, the extension opens with it - so you can start straight away.

1.  **Pick the Topic** you're collecting into

2.  **Highlight any text** - a snippet preview appears

3.  **Press Enter**, or click **Create**
