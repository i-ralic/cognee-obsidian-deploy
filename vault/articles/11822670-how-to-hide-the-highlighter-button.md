For the quick-open button to stop showing on every page whenever you highlight a piece of text, you should just:

1.  Open your Cluing Extension

2.  Press the ⚙️ icon on the top right

3.  Scroll down to the Preferences section

4.  Check the "Hide quick open button" setting item

![](/media/1632399860/image.webp)

After this Cluing highlighter button won't show anymore on the web pages you visit.

> [!note]
> **📌 Note:** You may need to refresh the page for the change to take effect.
