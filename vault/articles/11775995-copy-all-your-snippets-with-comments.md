You can easily copy all the snippets from any Topic - _comments included_ \- and paste them into tools like Google Docs, Word, Notion, or almost any other editor.

Here’s how:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  **Open the Topic** you want to copy from

3.  Click the three dots (**’’More options”**) in the top right corner

4.  Select **’’Copy Snippets’’**

5.  Paste the content wherever you need - it keeps both formatting and comments

Quick and convenient! 😄
