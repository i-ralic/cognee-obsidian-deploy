> [[collections/13877655-topics-buckets-clusters|Topics, Buckets, Clusters]] / [[collections/13953587-clusters|Clusters]]

To create a new Cluster on your Dashboard, click the **Add Cluster** button in the sidebar, under the **Cluster Groups** section.

If you don't see it there, you can also use the **Add Cluster** button **at the bottom** of the sidebar.

<video src="/media/2519346951/new-cluster-on-dash.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="1022" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Inside a Cluster, you can create **[[articles/11769093-create-a-new-topic-in-a-cluster|new Topics]]** and add snippets to them - or simply [[articles/11783231-how-to-move-snippets-to-another-topic|drag and drop]] snippets from your Inbox or other Topics into any Topic within the Cluster.

> [!warning]
> **_📌_** Currently, clusters can only be created on the Dashboard and can contain an **unlimited number** of topics. **_[[articles/11791468-what-subscription-plans-are-available-in-cluing|Free users]] can create up to 5 Clusters total._**
