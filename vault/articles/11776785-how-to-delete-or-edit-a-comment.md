Every comment you leave has three dots in the top-right corner.

Click them to open a menu where you can:

-   **Edit your comment**

-   **Delete your comment**

If you’ve mentioned someone, you’ll also see a third option: **Reply**. Clicking it will automatically add their name in the input field, so you don’t have to type it yourself.
