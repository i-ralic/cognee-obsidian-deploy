Before you start download the Cluing app - available for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

## **How to Open Share Settings on Mobile**

1.  Open the Cluing app and navigate to the Topic you want to share

2.  Tap the **three dots** in the top right corner

3.  Select **Share**

Here you'll see an overview of who's already involved in the Topic - the Cluster member, Topic owner, and any existing Topic members.

## How to Share a Topic

You can share a Topic in two ways:

## **Via Email**

Type in the email address of the person you want to invite and [[articles/11959751-what-different-roles-can-be-assigned-to-users|assign them a role]] - **Viewer, Commenter,** or **Editor**.

-   If the person already has a Cluing account, the Topic will appear in their **"Shared with Me"** Cluster

-   If they don't have an account yet, they'll receive an email invite to join

## **Via clu.ing Link**

Generate a [[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|clu.ing link]] and share it anywhere. You can assign a role to the link as well - **Viewer, Commenter,** or **Editor** - so everyone who opens it gets the right level of access.
