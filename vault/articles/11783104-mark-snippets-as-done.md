Instead of deleting snippets you've finished working on, you can now move them to a dedicated **"Completed" section**. This helps you maintain a clean and organized workspace, focusing only on what's active, without losing any of your past work.

## How to Mark Snippets as Done?

1.  Find the snippet you've completed in your Topic

2.  Open snippet menu (_three dots_)

3.  Click on the **"Mark as Done"** option

![](/media/2523181610/image.webp)

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note**: This moves the snippet to the "Completed" section.

## How to Access Completed Section?

To find snippets you've **marked as** **Done**:

-   Go to your **Dashboard**

-   Open the **topic menu** (_three dots_)

-   Click on **Completed**

Here you'll see all snippets you've archived.

> [!warning]
> **📌 _If you change your mind and want to mark the snippet as uncompleted, just click the three-dots menu and select “Uncomplete”._**
