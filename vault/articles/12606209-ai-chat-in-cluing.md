**Cluing Chat** gives you access to a suite of expert AI agents - **ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Claude, and DeepSeek**.

These agents help you research faster and get precise, relevant information, complete with sources. You can easily **switch between agents** depending on your task.

## How Chat Works

1.  Go to your **Dashboard.**

2.  In the **left sidebar**, click on **Chat.**

3.  A **search field** will appear.

4.  Choose an **Agent or Model** based on what you need

5.  **Type your prompt** and hit enter.

You’ll instantly get the **most relevant insights** from both the **web** and your **Cluing workspace**.

Each answer comes with **source links** for easy **verification**. 🔗

## Give Chat More Context

AI Chat gets sharper the more context you give it. You can ground a conversation in your own research and files in a few ways:

-   **[[articles/15444365-mention-a-topic-in-ai-chat|Mention a Topic]]** - Type **@** followed by a Topic name to pull in all the snippets and comments saved there. This gives Chat a unique context based on research you've already done.

-   **[[articles/15442354-upload-documents-to-ai-chat|Upload a document]]** - Click the upload button next to the input field to attach a **PDF** or other supported file type directly into the conversation. Ask the AI to summarize it, extract key points, compare it to other content, or answer specific questions - it'll use the file as context for more accurate answers.

> [!note]
> [[articles/15442354-upload-documents-to-ai-chat|Learn more about uploading documents →]]

-   **[[articles/15190105-upload-images-in-ai-chat|Upload an image]]** - Drop an image directly into the chat to ask questions about it or have the AI analyze its content.

-   **Combining these** - your saved Topics, an uploaded document, and a clear prompt - gives Chat everything it needs to give you an answer grounded in your actual research, not just the open web.

##
Turn Chat into a Document inside Canvas

Once you've gathered insights in Chat, instantly turn them into a structured, visual document by typing **[[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|"Open in Canvas"]]**.

This lets you see the big picture immediately and **refine your output** as needed - turning a conversation into something you can actually publish, share, or build on.

## Chat History

AI Chat History keeps all your past AI conversations in one dedicated place, so you never lose important ideas or answers.

What you can do with it:

1.  View all your previous AI chats

2.  Search to quickly find specific conversations

3.  Rename chats to keep things organized

4.  Delete conversations you no longer need
