Yes. 🙌🏼 The Cluing app is **free to download** on both [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

Some advanced features require a paid plan - the same plan you may already have on desktop. If you're on a paid plan, just sign in with the same account, and your subscription will carry over automatically.
