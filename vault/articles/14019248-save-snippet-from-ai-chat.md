You can save useful responses from your AI conversations as **snippets**. This allows you to keep valuable research and store them directly in your workspace.

## How to save a snippet from AI Chat

1.  Open any conversation from **AI Chat**

2.  Find response you like to save

3.  Choose the "**Save as Snippet"** option

4.  Select the topic where you want to store it

The snippet will appear inside the selected **topic**, where you can view, edit, and manage it like any other saved research.
