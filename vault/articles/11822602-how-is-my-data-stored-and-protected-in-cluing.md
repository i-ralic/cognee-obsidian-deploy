Your data in Cluing is stored and protected with the highest security standards.

All our infrastructure is hosted on **Microsoft Azure**, a globally recognized cloud platform that adheres to strict security practices. We implement best practices for data protection as well, which encompasses encryption at rest and in transit, strict access controls, and continuous monitoring for threats and vulnerabilities. Our **ISO27001** compliance signifies a comprehensive Information Security Management System (ISMS) is in place, covering all aspects of data security, risk management, and ongoing improvement.
