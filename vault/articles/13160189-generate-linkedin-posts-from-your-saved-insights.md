Writing LinkedIn posts that actually engage your audience starts long before you type a word. You need to **know who you’re writing for, what your audience cares about, which types of posts perform well, and what patterns you’ve noticed on LinkedIn**.

Then comes collecting ideas, tracking what’s working, and turning insights into actionable content.

## Save LinkedIn Posts With a Click

With Cluing, you can **[[articles/12638578-save-linkedin-posts-as-snippets|save any LinkedIn post]] with a single click** directly into your workspace.

You can **create topics** to organize them however you like - for example:

-   “LinkedIn Strategies That Deliver”

-   “B2B Marketing Insights 2025” or

-   “Influencer Marketing Tips”

This way, every insight remains safely stored in your workspace, **organized into topics for easy browsing and quick searching**.

![](/media/2508918189/image.webp)

## Use AI Chat to Draft LinkedIn Posts from Saved Insights

Once you’ve collected your inspiration, Cluing's **AI Chat** helps you extract actionable strategies from your saved posts.

> [!danger]
> **💡 [[articles/15800127-ai-agents-and-pro-ai-models-in-cluing-chat|Choose the Right AI Agent for Every Task]]
> ​**
> With Cluing Chat, you’re in control of which AI agent handles your research or content creation. Pick from **ChatGPT**, **Grok**, **Gemini**, **Claude**, or **DeepSeek**, or pick a custom agent tailored to your specific needs.
> ​
> This way, every task is handled by the AI best suited for the job.

Just ask the AI to gather all the key insights you need - **from your saved workspace or the web** - like engagement tactics, storytelling tips, posting patterns, or ideas tailored to your audience.

In seconds, you get a clear summary of what works, without scrolling through dozens of posts.

## Draft and Structure Posts with Canvas

Next, launch **[[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|AI Canvas]]** to visualize and structure your LinkedIn post. You can give a prompt like: _"Write a LinkedIn post on \[topic\] based on these strategies."_

The AI turns your collected insights into a polished draft, aligned with proven tactics and your audience’s interests. From there, you can:

-   **Refine tone and messaging directly in Canvas**

-   **Rearrange ideas for better flow**

-   **Add examples or personal touches**

Every insight remains traceable to the original post, so your content is **data-driven, verifiable, and aligned with what works on LinkedIn**.

![](/media/2508919118/image.webp)

## AI That Writes from Your Context, Not Generic Data

Cluing's AI doesn’t start from zero. It works **with everything you’ve already collected**.

Every post, article, or insight you’ve saved in your workspace becomes part of the AI’s context. That means the content it generates is **directly relevant to your strategy, audience, and goals**, not just generic advice.

> [!danger]
> 🔗 Every suggestion comes with **source links for easy verification**, so you always know where ideas came from and can trust the AI’s recommendations.

With AI that truly understands your saved insights, you can:

-   **Generate posts that hit the right audience:** Tailor your messaging based on patterns and strategies you’ve already observed.

-   **Stay consistent with your content strategy:** Every post aligns with your collected insights, keeping your LinkedIn presence coherent and professional.

-   **Save hours of work:** Instead of manually sorting through dozens of posts or notes, the AI organizes your ideas and drafts posts for you in minutes.

This is **AI built around your work**, not just another tool that gives generic outputs. It turns your research, saved strategies, and inspirations into actionable, high-quality LinkedIn posts - faster and smarter.
