If you're sure you no longer need a topic, you can delete it **permanently** from the Archive Cluster. Here’s how:

## **1\. Via the browser extension:**

-   Open the **Cluing extension**

-   From the dropdown menu, select the **Archive Cluster**

-   Scroll to find the topic you want to delete

-   Click the **delete button** next to the topic name

-   Confirm the deletion, and the topic will be permanently removed from your workspace

​

## **2\. Via the dashboard:**

-   Go to your Dashboard

-   Open the **Archive Cluster** from the side panel

-   Click on the topic you want to delete

-   In the top right corner, click the **three dots** (More options)

-   Choose **"Delete"** and confirm

> [!note]
> 📌 _**To delete a topic, you first need to [[articles/11748326-how-to-archive-a-topic|archive it]], and only then can you permanently remove it from the Archived cluster.**_

<video src="/media/2519325060/delete-topic---dashboard.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="1022" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

## Who can delete a Topic?

**In Team workspaces, only the topic owner** can delete a topic from the Archive cluster.

> [!warning]
> ⚠️ **Note:** Deleting a topic is permanent and cannot be undone.
