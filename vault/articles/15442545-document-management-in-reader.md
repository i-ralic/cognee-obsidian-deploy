Document Management gives you a central place to find and manage all your documents in Cluing - both uploaded Documents and saved web pages - without having to dig through your workspace to find them.

## **How to access it**

1.  Open the **Reader**

2.  Click the button in the **top left corner** of the Reader page

3.  Your document history will open with all your files in one place

Your document history shows all your saved content in one list - uploaded files and scraped web pages. From there you can:

-   **Open** any document directly in the Reader

-   **Delete** documents you no longer need

-   **Search** for a specific document using the search field at the top
