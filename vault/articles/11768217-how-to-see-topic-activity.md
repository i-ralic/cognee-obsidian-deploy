To view all Topic activity, go to your Dashboard, select the Topic, click the three dots (_More Options_) in the top right corner, and choose **Activity**.

There, you’ll see a timeline of all actions taken on that Topic and its snippets, including:

-   Who **created** a snippet

-   Who **deleted** a snippet

-   Who **changed** the snippet name

-   Who **liked** a snippet

-   Who **mentioned** you

It’s the easiest way to **stay updated** on what’s happening in your workspace.
