When you open **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]** on your Dashboard, switch to **Workspace Organizer** agent and tell the AI how you want to organize things.

![](/media/2532503058/image.webp)

For example, you can type a prompt like:

**_“Analyze my snippets from the inbox and sort them into the appropriate Topics.”_**

AI will go through your content and **automatically sort everything** for you - placing snippets into existing Topics or creating new Topics when needed.

This way, your **workspace stays organized** without manual sorting, so you can focus on working with your ideas and content.
