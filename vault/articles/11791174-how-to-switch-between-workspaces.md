To switch your Cluing Workspace, follow these steps:

## From the Extension

-   Open the Cluing extension.

-   Click the **topic drop-down menu**.

-   You’ll see the name of your current workspace - click on it.

-   A list of all workspaces you’re part of will appear.

-   Select the one you want to use.

Now, your new workspace is active and ready for saving insights.

## From the Dashboard

To switch Workspace from Dashboard, click on **the name** of your current workspace and select the one you want from the dropdown menu.
