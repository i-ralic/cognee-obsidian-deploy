## **Find a Topic on the Dashboard**

To find a specific Topic, open your Dashboard and use the search bar located in the **top right corner**.

Just type the Topic name, and the results will show as you type.

## **Find a Topic in the Extension**

When you open the Extension, you’ll see a search bar **at the top**. Type what you’re looking for, and you’ll find your Topic or snippet instantly.
