You can add collaborators directly to a topic using either the extension or your dashboard.

## **From the Extension:**

-   Open the extension and select a topic.

-   Click the **Share** button at the bottom.

-   Under **Topic Members**, enter the collaborator’s email address.

-   Set the permission level you want to assign.

The collaborator will receive an email and can work on the topic based on the permission you set.

![](/media/2499554207/image.webp)

## From the Dashboard

-   Go to your **Dashboard**.

-   Select the **Topic** you want to add someone to.

-   Click the **Share** button in the top right corner.

-   Under **Topic Members**, enter the collaborator’s email address.

-   Set the permission level you want to assign.

The collaborator will receive an **email invitation** and can work on the topic according to the permission you set.

![](/media/2499570251/image.webp)

> [!danger]
> 📌 When you add someone to a Topic, it will appear in their **Personal Workspace**, under the **[[articles/11792863-topics-and-clusters-shared-with-you|Shared Topics]]** cluster.

![](/media/1625230238/image.webp)
