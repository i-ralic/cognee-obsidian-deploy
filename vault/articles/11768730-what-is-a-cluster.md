In Cluing, a Cluster is essentially a **collection of related Topics**. Think of it as a higher-level organizational tool.

## Several types of clusters

There are a few types of clusters you can see on your workspace

1.  **My Topics** \- Default Cluster

2.  **Clusters you create** \- You name them as you like

3.  **[[articles/11748326-how-to-archive-a-topic|Archive Cluster]]** \- A place where all archived Topics go to

4.  **Clusters shared with you** \- Clusters you were added to

![](/media/1634258581/image.webp)

## Why use Clusters?

-   **Clear structure** - Clusters create a clear and logical structure for your research, making it easier to find what you need

-   **Manage large projects** \- Clusters help you break down large projects into manageable chunks

-   **Improve Collaboration** - Clusters make it easier for your team to understand the scope of your research and find relevant information
