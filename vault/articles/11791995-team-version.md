If you've been using Cluing on a Hobbyist or Professional plan and want to start collaborating with your team, **upgrading to the Team Version** is the way to go.

## How to get started with Team Version?

Head to your Dashboard's Workspace Settings menu and click **Upgrade to Team**. Inside **Cluing for Teams** section click **Create a Team.** You'll be guided through a quick **[5-step onboarding process](https://app.cluing.io/onboarding)**:

![](/media/2544500765/image-33.webp)

1.  **Name your team** and choose an icon

2.  **Select your team's areas of interest** to personalize your experience

3.  **Invite your first team members** by entering their email addresses - you can start with just two and add more members later

4.  **Choose your billing period**

5.  **Complete your purchase**

![](/media/2502231104/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> 💡 **The Team Version** **simplifies collaboration** by allowing you to add all your team members to a single workspace. This ensures everyone has access to all Clusters/Topics within that workspace, eliminating the need to share each topic individually.
