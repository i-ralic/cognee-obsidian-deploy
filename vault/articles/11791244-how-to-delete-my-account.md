> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/13994794-workspace-and-account-settings|Workspace and Account Settings]]

To delete your account, go to the **Dashboard** and click on your **initials** in the top right corner.

Then select **Account Settings**, scroll all the way down, and you’ll see the option **Delete my account**.

<video src="/media/2519116115/delete-account.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="986" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

> [!danger]
> 📌 **NOTE**: Deleting your account will permanently erase all your information and subscriptions.
