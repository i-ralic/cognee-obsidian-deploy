When you click the ''**+''** button in the extension to create a new Topic, you’ll see two options:

-   ✔️ to confirm and [[articles/11748302-how-to-create-a-new-topic|create the Topic]]

-   🗑️ to undo and cancel

If you change your mind, just click 🗑️ to stop it - no more accidental Topics cluttering your workspace! 🎉
