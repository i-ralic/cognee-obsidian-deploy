Cluing lets you turn any TikTok link into a **snippet**. Once added, the content is stored in your workspace, where you can organize it, revisit it, and use it like any other saved resource.

## On Mobile

1.  Open TikTok and tap **Share**

2.  Click "more" button in options

3.  Select the **Cluing app** from the options displayed

4.  You’ll be redirected to the Cluing app

5.  A modal will appear where you can **confirm and save the snippet**

> [!warning]
> Note: To save on mobile you need to [[articles/13833323-cluing-mobile-apps|install]] Cluing mobile app

## On Desktop

1.  Copy the **TikTok post or profile link**

2.  Go to your **Dashboard**

3.  Paste the link into the **“Add Link”** section

4.  The content will be automatically saved as a **snippet**
