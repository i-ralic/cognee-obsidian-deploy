Need help using Cluing?
Just ask AI Chat. It finds the relevant Help Center articles, reads them, and gives you a clear, direct answer - with links to the articles so you can dig deeper.

Whether you're trying to figure out how something works or looking for the fastest way to complete a task, you'll get an answer in seconds.

## **How to get help**

1.  Open **AI Chat** in the Cluing desktop app or mobile app.

2.  Ask a how-to or support question, like _"How do I share a topic?"_ or _"How do I save a PDF?"_

3.  AI Chat answers based on the **Help Center** and links the articles it used.
