To move snippets to external sources, simply click and hold on the snippet, then drag it to your desired location.

You can do it from both the **Dashboard** and the **Extension**.

You can Drag&Drop Snippets to:

-   Google Docs

-   Notion

-   Gmail

-   Outlook, and a lot more supported apps.
