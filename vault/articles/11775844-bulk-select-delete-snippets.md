Open the Topic where you want to delete snippets and follow these steps:

**Step 1: Press SHIFT to Select**

-   Hold the **SHIFT** key to show checkboxes on snippets. Then select the snippets you want.

**Step 2: Delete**

-   Click the **Delete** button to remove the selected snippets. A confirmation will ask you to confirm before deleting.

> [!danger]
> **📌 Note:** Deleted snippets can’t be recovered.
>
> To avoid accidents, you’ll get a quick **“Are you sure?”** confirmation before deleting any snippet.
