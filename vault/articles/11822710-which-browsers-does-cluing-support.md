Cluing works on:

-   **[Chrome Webstore](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/collabwriting-shareable-n/jjefmmjicedljonbpdoeceklnhobmolo)** - For Chrome, Opera, Brave, Arc (and other Chromium-based browsers)

-   **[Edge Add-Ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/collabwriting-shareable/oaaidbemenheminahjaojcafppklekjd)**

    -   Both Android and IOS users can now use the extension [[articles/11822721-how-to-get-the-cluing-mobile-extension|on mobile]]

-   **[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/collabwriting-shareable-notes/)** - Grab it from the Firefox Add-ons store and make saving and organizing info way easier.

    -   📱 **Bonus for Android users**: it works on your phone too
