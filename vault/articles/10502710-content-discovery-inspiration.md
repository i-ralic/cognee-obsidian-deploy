Let's face it - scrolling through social media, blogs, and articles eats up **_hours_** of your day (**over 2.5 hours on average!**). And while you stumble on amazing ideas, quotes, or stats you _swear_ you'll revisit later, they vanish into the endless feed.

By tomorrow, finding that important piece of information feels like searching through a mountain of content.

Now imagine a tool that allows you to **save _anything_ inspiring in seconds** - and actually find it again.

With Cluing there are no more frantic searches or lost time - save anything, anywhere.

-   **Highlight** text on blogs, **articles**, or even **PDFs** and save it instantly with our browser extension.

-   **[[articles/11784694-capture-content-from-social-media-on-desktop|One-click saves]]:** Found something useful on LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Reddit, Quora, Substack, or TikTok? Click once to save it - forget screenshots and bookmarks.

-   **Add your thoughts:** Leave comments directly on the content you've saved.

-   **Share it** with teammates, clients, or friends. Brainstorm together or gather content - right inside your collection.

-   You can also **[[articles/11775739-drag-drop-snippets-to-external-sources|export it to Google Docs]]** for further editing and collaboration.

## Save from Anywhere - On Any Platform

Inspiration doesn't wait for you to be at your desk. Cluing works wherever you find it.

**On Desktop**, the browser extension puts a Save button directly under posts on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, YouTube, and Quora. One click captures the full content - not just a link - so it survives even if the original gets deleted.

👉🏼 [[articles/11784694-capture-content-from-social-media-on-desktop|Capture Content from Social Media on Desktop]]﹒[[articles/11784650-capture-youtube-videos|Capture YouTube Videos]]

**On Mobile**, the **[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US) app** makes saving even easier - including from platforms like TikTok and Substack. Just use the native **Share** button from any app and select **Cluing**. The post lands directly in your Topic, ready to use.

👉🏼 [[articles/15654106-save-tiktok-videos-to-cluing-on-mobile|Save TikTok Videos to Cluing on Mobile]]

## Read and Highlight on Mobile

If you prefer to browse in a mobile browser and highlight specific text, install the Cluing extension on **[[articles/11839924-mobile-extension-on-microsoft-edge|Edge or Firefox for mobile]]**. It's the same highlight-and-save flow you know from desktop, just on your phone.

You can also open any saved article in Cluing's built-in **[[articles/12267724-cluing-reader|Reader]]** - a clean, distraction-free view with no ads or cookie banners - and highlight directly from there.

Reader is available on mobile too, so you can read and capture insights from anywhere.

> [!note]
> _💡 Everything you save on mobile syncs instantly across all your devices._

## Don't Have Time to Read Everything? Summarize It.

Found an article that looks promising but don't have time to read it in full? The **[[articles/14211688-summarize-webpage|Summarize Webpage]]** feature does it for you in seconds.

Cluing's AI scans the entire page, pulls out the most important points, and automatically creates a snippet in your Topic so you capture the insight without reading word for word.

You can trigger it in three ways: from the extension while browsing, from the highlighter menu when you select any text, or by pasting a link directly into an empty Topic.
