Easily save content by adding a link **directly to your Topic**.

## **Here's how to create a snippet from a link:**

1.  Go to your **Dashboard** and open the Topic where you want to save the link.

2.  Click the **Add Snippets** button next to the Topic title.

3.  Select **Add Link** from the options.

4.  Paste the URL of the page you want to save and press **Save**.

This creates **a [[articles/11783251-bookmarks-save-now-read-later|bookmark]] snippet** in your Topic for quick access later.
