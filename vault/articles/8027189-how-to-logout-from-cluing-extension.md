To logout of the Extension click ⚙️ icon on top of the extension to open Extension Settings.

![](/media/1995156532/image.webp)

Then scroll to the bottom of Settings Panel and Logout.

![](/media/1995162463/image.webp)

You should then see the Extension's Login Panel

![](/media/1995173754/image.webp)
