The Slash Command is a shortcut designed to keep you in the "flow" while writing. Instead of searching through toolbars, you can format your text and add media directly from your keyboard.

## How it's working?

To use it, simply type '**/**' in your comment. A **drop-down menu** will appear, and you will have **multiple options to choose from**. You can use your mouse to click an option or use the **arrow keys** on your keyboard to navigate.

## Available options

The menu allows you to quickly insert:

-   **Media:** Upload images

-   **Formatting:** Instantly start bulleted or numbered lists.

-   **Collaboration:** Use **[[articles/11793832-who-can-be-mentioned-in-the-comments|mentions]]** to notify a teammate or tags to organize your thoughts.
