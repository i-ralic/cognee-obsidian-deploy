The **Move To** feature allows you to transfer a Snippet and Topics from one Topic/Cluster to another.

It's a simple way to keep your projects tidy by making sure every Snippet and Topic is in the right place.

### Here's how to move snippet from one topic to another:

1.  Click the snippet menu (**three dots**).

2.  Select **Move To**.

3.  Choose the destination topic where you want the snippet to go.

<video src="/media/2519387693/move-to.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="982" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

That's it 🎊 Your snippet is now moved to the right place, helping you keep projects organized.

## Here's how to move a Topic from one Cluster to another:

1.  Click the Topic menu (**three dots**).

2.  Select **Move To**.

3.  Choose the destination Cluster where you want the Topic to go.
