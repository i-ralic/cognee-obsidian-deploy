Cluing offers flexible plans to fit your needs, whether you're working solo or with a team:

## **Hobbyist** \- $0 (Free forever)

-   Easily save, organize, and comment on content - a solid way to get started and keep your insights in one place

## Professional - $20/month

-   Great for individuals who read and research a lot and want more control, context, and features

## **[[articles/11791995-team-version|Team]]** - $25/month per user

-   Made for teams who work together on research, content, or strategy and need a shared space to stay aligned

## Agent Seats - 25/month per user

-   Each seat unlocks an Autonomous Agent

-   One Agent = one Seat

-   $25 / month per Agent on Professional and Team plans

-   Charged to the same payment method as the rest of your workspace

-   Included with every Seat - Unlimited Skills and Connectors

## Add-ons: Credits - $12/month for 1000 credits

Credits fuel the actual work Agents do. Buy them at registration, or top up later from the **workspace menu → AI Credits**.

Extra credits sit on top of anything included with your plan.

> [!tip]
> 🎁 Pro and Team subscriptions have 2,500 AI Credits gitf every month. A $300/year value. Runs on top of any plan, no action needed.
