While browsing on your phone, you can save content from almost anywhere - webpages, **YouTube**, **X**, **LinkedIn**, **TikTok**, and more - directly to Cluing as a snippet, without switching apps or copying links manually.

> [!warning]
> 📍 Before you start **download the Cluing mobile app** - available for
> **[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072)** and **[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).**

## **How to Save**

1.  Open any content you want to save in your mobile browser or app

2.  Tap the **Share** button

3.  Find and select **Cluing** from the list of apps

![Use your phone's Share button to send any content straight to Cluing - Webpage, video, or social post](/media/2499889928/image.webp)

## **What Happens Next**

Cluing will automatically generate a snippet from the content. From there, you can:

-   Choose a **Topic** (and Cluster) where you want to save it

-   Edit the **title**

-   Add a **comment** or **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|mention someone]]**

-   Edit the **link** or the **auto-generated content** if needed

-   Tap '**Create'** and the snippet is saved to your Topic.
