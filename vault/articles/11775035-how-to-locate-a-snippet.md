> [[collections/13970558-snippets-comments-tags|Snippets, Comments, Tags]] / [[collections/13970600-snippets|Snippets]]

Under every saved snippet, you’ll find the **Locate** button.

When you click **Locate**, it will take you exactly to the original piece of text on the webpage or PDF where the snippet was saved.

The text will be **highlighted**, so you can quickly see the context without searching for it yourself. This makes it easy to revisit the source and understand the snippet better.
