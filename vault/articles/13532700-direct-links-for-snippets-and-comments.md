You can generate direct URLs that point colleagues to a specific snippet or a single comment within a snippet.

## **How to Share?**

1.  Find a Snippet or Comment you want to share

2.  Open the "**More Options"** menu

3.  Click "**Copy Share Link**"

Now, you have copied shareable link to that specific snippet or comment. Sent it to your collaborators and it will redirect them directly to that snippet!

![](/media/1994980875/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> Note: To ensure your data stays secure, the link will only work for users who already have permission on that topic. If the General Access is set to Public then anyone will be able to open that particular link if they have Cluing account.
