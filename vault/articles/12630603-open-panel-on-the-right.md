By default, the Cluing Extension panel opens on the right side of your browser. If you prefer to have it on the left, you can change this in the settings.

## **How to change panel position**

1.  Open the Cluing Extension in your browser

2.  Click on the **Settings** button in the top-right corner

3.  Find the **Open Panel on the Right** option

4.  Toggle it off to move the panel to the left

<video src="/media/2533085630/ExtOnTheRight.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1906" height="908" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Once changed, the panel will open on your chosen side automatically.
