While watching a YouTube video, you can **add timestamps** directly inside your snippet comments.

Just type something like “check out 5:50”, and it will automatically turn into **a clickable link**. Click it anytime, and the video will jump straight to that exact moment.

This makes it easy to navigate key moments inside a video and quickly revisit the most important parts of your notes.

> [!warning]
> **💡 Tip:** When saving YouTube [[articles/15645950-everything-you-can-do-with-the-cluing-mobile-app|videos from your phone]], add timestamps in the snippet comments as you go. This makes it easy to revisit the exact moments that matter later.
