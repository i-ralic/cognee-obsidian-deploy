When you open the Cluing extension and click the small **gear icon** (⚙️) in the top right corner, you’ll open **Settings** - this is where you can customize how the extension works for you.

## **Open on Startup**

**Choose what opens by default when you launch the** Cluing **extension.**

Under **Open on Startup**, you can choose what the extension should open by default:

-   **[[articles/12638164-open-on-startup#:~:text=and%20save%20time. ,Open%20Inbox%20on%20Startup, If%20you%20want|Inbox]]** - Opens your Inbox, a quick-drop space where you can save snippets instantly and organize them later.

-   **[[articles/12638164-open-on-startup#:~:text=and%20new%20snippets. ,Open%20Your%20Last%20Viewed%20Topic%20on%20Startup, If%20you%20want|My Last Viewed Topic]]** - Reopens the last topic you were working on, helping you pick up right where you left off.

-   **[[articles/12638164-open-on-startup#:~:text=any%20extra%20clicks. ,Open%20the%20Newest%20Topic%20on%20This%20Link%20on%20Startup, If%20you%20want|Newest Topic on this Link]]** - Opens the most recently created Topic for the current page, or if none exists, it opens **the oldest one** available.

This way, you can set up the extension to match your workflow and save time.

## **General preferences**

Other things you can customize:

1.  **[[articles/12630603-open-panel-on-the-right|Open panel on the right]]** - choose if you want the extension to open on the right or the left side of your screen.

2.  **[[articles/12630550-keep-extension-open-on-every-page|Keep open on every page]] -** if enabled, the extension will stay open on every webpage you visit.

3.  **[[articles/11822670-how-to-hide-the-highlighter-button|Hide the highlighter button]] -** decide whether the highlighter button should pop up every time you highlight something. Having this option on is useful during deep research for faster insight collection

4.  **[[articles/12621034-snippets-saving-preference|Add cards to the bottom]]** - Select if you want your snippets to be added at the bottom of the list or the top. This will be replicated in how they're seen in the Dashboard.

5.  **[[articles/12629957-focus-on-bookmark|Focus Bookmark on Startup]]** - It's a preference that will automatically highlight your bookmark snippet when you open the extension on that topic (if a bookmark exists). Helpful if there are general notes to keep in mind on a certain topic, document, or video.

6.  **[[articles/11823211-show-all-highlights-reverse-locate|Show All Highlights]] -** When this option is turned on, all your created highlights will appear directly on the webpage as you browse.

## Experimental Features

Turning on **Experimental Features** gives you early access to tools we’re still testing, including:

-   **Resize Page** - Adjust how the extension panel fits your screen.

-   **[[articles/12630200-most-highlighted|Most Highlighted]] -** Turning on this preference lets you see which parts of a page other Cluing users have highlighted the most. This gives you a quick overview of key content, helping you focus on the most valuable information right away.

-   **[[articles/12701821-knowledge-triggers|Knowledge Triggers]]** - Automatically identifies saved research on any webpage you visit. Click any underlined text to jump directly to its original Snippet and Topic.
