When someone leaves a comment and mentions you, replying is super simple!

Just click the **three dots** on that comment and select **“Reply.”** This will automatically tag the person who wrote the original comment, so your response reaches them directly.

<video src="/media/1810422501/reply-to-a-comment.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="982" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

It’s an easy way to keep the conversation clear and make sure the right people see your feedback. 🙌🏼
