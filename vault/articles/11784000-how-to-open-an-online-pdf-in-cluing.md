An online PDF can be opened with **Cluing** **through the Dashboard**, as well as directly **through the extension**.

## Opening a PDF through the extension

To open an **online PDF** directly in Cluing, follow these steps:

1.  Click the Cluing extension shortcut while viewing online PDF.

2.  It will automatically open a PDF in the Cluing Reader, ready for you to highlight and add notes.

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note**: If you open a PDF directly from your computer in the browser, you can't use it this way. You'll need to **[[articles/11784127-how-to-upload-pdfs-to-your-workspace|upload the PDF]]** instead.

## Opening a PDF through the Dashboard

You can open an online PDF in two ways from the Dashboard:

**#1 Using the ''Reader'' Button**

-   On the left sidebar, click **[[articles/12267724-cluing-reader|Reader]]**

![](/media/2499684683/image.webp)

-   Paste the PDF link into the field provided.

-   Click **Open from URL**.

<video src="/media/2468073821/uploadfromlinkpdf.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="906" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

**#2 Using the Quick Add Button**

-   Within any Topic, click the **Quick Add button**.

-   Choose to add a link and paste the PDF URL there to open it directly.

<video src="/media/1937154585/AddPDFThroughTopic.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1904" height="906" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Once your PDF is in, it becomes part of your workspace. You can then **highlight, comment, and collaborate** on its content.
