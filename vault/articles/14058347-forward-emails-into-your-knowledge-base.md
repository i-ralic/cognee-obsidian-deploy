Instead of letting useful information get lost in your inbox, you can **store, organize, and reuse it** inside your workspace. Simply forward any email directly into your workspace and automatically turn it into a **structured snippet**.

## **How to set it up**

1.  Open workspace settings

2.  Navigate to "Emails" section

3.  Click to add a new email

4.  Choose Topic

5.  The system will generate a unique address

## How It Works

Each Topic in your workspace has its own **[[articles/14354712-custom-domain-and-email-address-setup-pro-team|unique email address]]**.

You can use this address in two ways:

-   **Forward existing emails** that contain useful information you want to save in Cluing.

-   **Subscribe to newsletters or email updates** using your Cluing email address and have new content automatically added to your workspace.

Whenever an email reaches your Topic's address, Cluing will:

-   Receive the email content

-   Process it automatically

-   Create a **new snippet inside the selected Topic with summary of an email**

This way, important information from your inbox can become organized, searchable knowledge in your workspace.

## Review and Highlight

When you click **Locate**, the email content opens in the **[[articles/12267724-cluing-reader|Reader page]]**.

There you can:

-   Read the full email in a clean format

-   Highlight important information

-   Save highlighted sections as **separate snippets**

This lets you quickly **extract important information from emails and save it as reusable snippets.**
