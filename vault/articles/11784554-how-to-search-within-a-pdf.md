**Search in PDF** helps you quickly find exactly what you need. Here’s how:

-   Open any PDF.

-   At the top of your document, you’ll see the **search bar**.

-   Type your word or phrase - all matches will be instantly **highlighted**.

From there, you can either **save a snippet** or simply keep reading - it’s up to you! 😊
