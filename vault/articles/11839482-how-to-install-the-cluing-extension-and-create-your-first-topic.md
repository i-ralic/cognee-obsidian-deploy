## First step - Install the extension

The Cluing browser extension is used for creating snippets on different web pages, YouTube videos, or social media like LinkedIn or X.

To install the Cluing browser extension, you can go to **[Chrome Webstore](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cluing-ai-knowledge-base/jjefmmjicedljonbpdoeceklnhobmolo),** the **[Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/cluing-shareable-notes-/oaaidbemenheminahjaojcafppklekjd),** or the **[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/collabwriting-shareable-notes/)** store. Find Cluing and then just click **Add to Chrome/Edge/Firefox** button

🎊 That’s it, you’ve just successfully installed our browser extension. Don't forget to **pin it** for easy access.

## Topics and Snippets explained

**[[articles/11774653-what-is-a-snippet|Snippets]]** are the core of Cluing - they can be anything from a highlight created on a webpage, a saved post from LinkedIn, or even a snapshot of a YouTube video.

Always **locatable**, always **collaborative**.

**[[articles/11748259-what-is-a-topic|Topics]]** are where you organize your snippets from different pages and sources, and they can further be organized into **[[articles/11768730-what-is-a-cluster|Clusters]]**.

![](/media/2502109549/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> _💡 If you are researching the best Chrome extensions, you could find one mentioned in the New York Times, a newly launched one on TechCrunch, and one more suggested on the Forbes website. All those snippets you can organize in a single **Topic,** you might name “The best Chrome extensions of the year.”_

## Create, add comments, and share your first Topic

The Cluing extension isn't just a place where you can save quick snippets, but also a place where you can create new topics, attach your own insights in the form of comments, and share them with members of your team.
​
Here's how to do it:

1.  To create a new topic, click the **+ icon** in the top right corner of the **extension.**

2.  Then, **select any piece of text** on the webpage, and it will appear as a highlight in the extension. To create a new snippet, just **press “Enter”** or **click the “Create”** button.

3.  To **leave comments** on your snippet, you just **click the Comment** button, add any text, link, or screenshot, and then **press “Enter”** or **click the “Comment”** button once again.

4.  After your Topic is created, you can share it by **first setting up the permissions** and **[[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|copying the link at the bottom]]** of the sidebar.

> [!warning]
> 📍 Instead of sharing a link, you can add a collaborator by typing their **email address** directly inside the extension. Assign them a role - Viewer, Commenter, or Editor - and they'll get **immediate access to the Topic**.

## The Cluing Dashboard

To go to your dashboard, where you can further **organize your snippets and topics**, simply click the button located at the bottom right of the extension.

![](/media/2502132035/image.webp)

Once you click it, [your Dashboard](https://app.cluing.io/) with all the previously collected snippets will open.
