**_Only mention_** **lets you mention people without sharing the entire Topic with them.**

This way, they’ll receive an **email notification** with the snippet details and a link to **locate the original source**.

![](/media/2519184374/image.webp)

## **📌 Important note for sharing from your Inbox:**

When you mention someone under a snippet in your **Inbox**, **Mention only is the only available option**.

This means the person will receive that single snippet by email, but **will not get access to your full Inbox or any Topics**.

![](/media/2519190404/image.webp)
