To create a new topic inside a cluster from the extension, follow these steps:

-   Click the **drop-down menu** where all your clusters and topics are listed.

-   Find the **cluster** where you want to create a new topic.

-   Click the **\+ button** next to the cluster name.

-   **Edit or save the topic name** as needed.

Your new topic will be created inside that cluster.

<video src="/media/1995217784/ClusterTopicExtCluing.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1908" height="906" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

If you forget this stop, don't worry - you can always just [[articles/11768652-reordering-topics|drag and drop]] it into the right cluster through the Dashboard.
