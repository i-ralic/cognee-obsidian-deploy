Cluing lets you save any TikTok video directly to your workspace in just a few taps. Once saved, you can organize it alongside the rest of your research, add comments, tags, and revisit it anytime.

To save TikTok videos on mobile, you'll need to have the Cluing app installed - available for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

## **How to Save a TikTok Video**

1.  Open **TikTok** and find the video you want to save

2.  Tap the **Share** button

3.  Tap **More** in the sharing options

4.  Select **Cluing** from the list of apps

5.  You'll be redirected to the Cluing app

6.  A window will appear where you can choose a Topic, add a comment or #tag - then tap **Save**
