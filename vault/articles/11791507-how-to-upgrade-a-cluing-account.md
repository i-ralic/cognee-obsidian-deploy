> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/14010526-subscriptions-plans-and-payments|Subscriptions, Plans and Payments]]

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**.

2.  Click on your **initials** in the top right corner.

3.  Select **Manage Payment Plan** from the menu.

4.  There, you’ll see what’s included in each plan as well as the prices.

5.  Choose the plan you want and upgrade your account.

> [!danger]
> 📌 **Note:** When a team plan subscription expires, only the **workspace owner** can renew it!
