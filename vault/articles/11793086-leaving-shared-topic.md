## From the Extension

1.  Open the extension and open the drop-down menu with all your topics.

2.  Scroll all the way down to find the **Shared Topics** cluster.

3.  Click the **X button** next to the topic you want to leave.

## From the Dashboard

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**.

2.  Open the topic shared with you that you want to leave.

3.  Click the **topic menu option** (three dots in the top right corner).

4.  Select **Leave**.
