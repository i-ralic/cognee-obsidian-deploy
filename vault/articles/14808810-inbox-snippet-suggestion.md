Stop wasting time manually sorting your inbox. You can use **AI Suggestions** button on your snippets, making it easier to categorize, label, and clean up your workspace in seconds.

## **How to Use AI Suggestions**

1.  Open your **Inbox Topic**

2.  Look for the **AI icon** located in the bottom-left corner of snippets

3.  Click the button to reveal three smart, content-based actions

    1.  **Add Title:** Automatically creates a snippet title \[if you haven't added one\]

    2.  **Add Tag:** Suggests relevant #tags for easier searching

    3.  **Move to Topic:** Based on your previous research, the AI suggests the best Topic for your snippet.

> [!warning]
> **📌 Note:** Clicking "Move to Topic" transfers the snippet out of your Inbox immediately, which will close the suggestions menu.
