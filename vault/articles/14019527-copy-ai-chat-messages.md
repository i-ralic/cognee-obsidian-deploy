You can easily copy messages from AI Chat and place them on your clipboard. This allows you to quickly reuse AI responses in documents, emails, notes, or other tools.

## How to copy a message?

1.  Open **AI Chat**

2.  Find the AI message you want to copy

3.  Click the "**Copy message"** option

Once the message is copied, you can paste it anywhere you need, such as documents, notes, emails, or other tools you use in your workflow.
