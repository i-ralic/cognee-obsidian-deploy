With the Cluing mobile extension, you can:

-   **Highlight key information from articles/reports.**

-   **Add comments and insights directly to saved snippets.**

-   **Collaborate with your team in real time.**

-   **Keep track of sources and organize research into structured knowledge bases.**

## How to get the Cluing mobile extension (Android)

To get the Cluing mobile extension on Microsoft Edge, follow these steps:

1.  **Open the Edge menu**: Click the button in the bottom right corner to open the menu.

2.  **Open the extensions list**: In the menu, find the **Extensions** option.

3.  **Scroll down to** Cluing: Scroll through the list of extensions until you find Cluing.

![](/media/2008894760/Edge-Extension.webp)

With just 3 simple steps, you'll have the extension on your phone and can start gathering information on the go.

## How to get the Cluing mobile extension (IOS)

1.  Open the **Edge browser** on your phone.

2.  Tap the **three lines (_menu options_)** in the bottom right corner.

3.  Select **Extensions**.

4.  Find Cluing in the list and tap **Get**.

![](/media/2009005620/Edge-Ext-2.webp)

## **Saving Snippets with the Edge Mobile Extension**

Open any webpage and **highlight any text** - a small menu will appear with three options:

-   **Save** it as a Snippet

-   **Bookmark** the entire page, or

-   **Summarize** the whole article

To save time, Cluing's Smart Assistant analyzes your previous research and automatically suggests the best **Topic**, **Title**, and **Tags** for each new snippet.

In most cases, all you need to do is confirm and move on.
