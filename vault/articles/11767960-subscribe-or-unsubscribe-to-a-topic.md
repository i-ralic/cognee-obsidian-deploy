Want to get notified when someone adds or changes something in a Topic?

Every topic on the dashboard has a little bell icon in the **top-right corner**.

Click it to **subscribe or unsubscribe** from that Topic, and choose whether you want to receive updates.

![](/media/2511898303/image.webp)

It’s an easy way to **stay in the loop** for topics that interest you the most. 🔔

## Default Subscription Rules

When you are involved with a Topic, the system handles your initial subscription status automatically, based on how you were added:

-   **Create a Topic:** You are **subscribed** to that Topic by default

-   **Added to an existing Topic:** You are **unsubscribed** by default

## Receiving Notifications While Unsubscribed

If you decide to keep a Topic **unsubscribed**, you will still receive notifications in one specific ocasion:

-   When someone directly [[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|mentions]] you in the Topic using **@mention**
