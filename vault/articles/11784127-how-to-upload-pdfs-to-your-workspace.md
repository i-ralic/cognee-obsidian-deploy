Got a PDF sitting on your computer - a report, a paper, an ebook?
Upload it to Cluing and you can highlight it, comment on it, and save snippets from it, just like any webpage.
​
To upload a PDF directly from your computer:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Click **Reader** in the left sidebar

3.  Select **Upload Document**

4.  Pick the PDF file from your computer

![](/media/2470544455/image.webp)

## Choose how it's saved

Before the upload finishes, Cluing asks how you want to read the file:

-   **Text Document** - converts the PDF into clean, structured text. Best for text-heavy reading like papers, reports, and ebooks.

-   **Regular PDF** - keeps the original layout exactly as it is in the file. Best when the layout carries meaning: slide decks, scans, invoices, anything with charts or columns.

![](/media/2575926159/TextPDF.webp)

Once the upload is done, your Document opens in Reader, and it's yours to work with - you can [[articles/12657586-how-to-add-annotations-to-documents-on-desktop|start working on your document]], the same way you would on any webpage.

It also stays put. Every file you upload is kept in your [[articles/15442545-document-management-in-reader|document history]], so you can reopen it weeks later without hunting for the original on your hard drive.
