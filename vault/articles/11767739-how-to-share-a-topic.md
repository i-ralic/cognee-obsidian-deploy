## 📌 Share a Topic from the Extension

You can share your Topic directly while working in the Cluing extension:

**Option #1: Copy the Clu.ing Link**
At the bottom of your extension, you'll find the **[[articles/11822856-what-s-a-clu-ing-link|clu.ing link]]**.

-   Choose the permission level (Viewer, Commenter, or Editor).

-   Copy the link and send it to your collaborator.

**Option #2: Add Collaborator by Email**

-   Instead of sending a link, you can directly add your collaborator's **email address** inside the extension.

-   Set the appropriate role (**Viewer, Commenter, or Editor**), and they’ll get immediate access.

![](/media/1998723427/image.webp)

## 📌 Share a Topic from the Dashboard

1.  Open the Topic you want to share.

2.  Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner.

3.  From the share panel, you can:

-   **Copy and share the Clu.ing link** (choose permission level).

-   **Invite people via email** and assign them a role (Viewer, Commenter, or Editor).

![](/media/2511888531/image.webp)

Everything you need for controlled sharing is in one place. 🙌🏼
