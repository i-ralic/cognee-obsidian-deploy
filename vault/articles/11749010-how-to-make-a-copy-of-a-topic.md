Want to reuse your work or need to share it with a new set of collaborators? You can easily copy an entire topic to **another workspace** or a **different cluster** within the same workspace.

**Here's how:**

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Open the Topic you want to duplicate

3.  Open the Topic Menu (_click the three dots_)

4.  Click the **"Duplicate to"** button

5.  Choose where you want to copy it (_either to a [[articles/11768446-how-to-copy-a-topic-to-another-workspace|different workspace]] or to another cluster within the current workspace_)

6.  Choose whether you want to **include comments**
