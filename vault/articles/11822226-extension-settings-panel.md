When you open the Cluing extension, look in the top right corner - you’ll see a small gear icon (⚙️). That’s your Settings panel, and it lets you customize how the extension works for you.

![](/media/2528651674/Extension-settings-panel.webp)

## Here you’ll find quick access to:

1.  **Helpful links** - anything and everything you'd like to learn about Cluing, quick links to give us your feedback, and access to our help desk. There is also quick access to [[articles/15551899-cluing-ai-chat-models-vs-chat-agents|Cluing AI]] and [[articles/15191401-explore-your-knowledge-graphs|Knowledge Graphs]] to get you there faster.

2.  **Legal** - here you can check out our terms of service, as well as the privacy and refund policies

3.  **Open on startup** - choose your most practical start, for some it can be [[articles/11748278-what-is-the-cluing-inbox|Inbox]] for all the unsorted thoughts, but you can also choose to always see your last viewed topic or the topic that was most recently created on the link/document you're visiting

4.  **[[articles/11822407-adjusting-extension-preferences|Preferences]]** - the place to make your Cluing extension work for you. Where the panel will go, are snippets saved on top or bottom, will the extension be opened on every page, all up to you.

5.  **Plan** \- here you can check in on your Cluing plan and see when your licence expires

6.  **[[articles/11791174-how-to-switch-between-workspaces|Workspace]]** \- if you're a part of multiple teams or just want to switch between your personal and team plan, you can do it here with just a click

7.  **Actions -** here you can choose to **[[articles/8027189-how-to-logout-from-cluing-extension|log out of your extension]]** (but we hope to see you back soon 💛)
