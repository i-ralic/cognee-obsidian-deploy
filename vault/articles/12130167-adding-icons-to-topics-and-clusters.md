## How to add emojis to Topics and Clusters?

You can now add icons to your Topics and Clusters to **visually organize** your workspace. This makes it super easy to quickly spot your projects in the sidebar and make them stand out.

### Here’s how to do it:

-   Go to your Topic or Cluster.

-   Click on the icon button in front of the name.

-   An emoji picker will open.

-   Choose your favorite emoji.

-   Done!

Your Topic/Cluster now has its own **visual identity** 🎉
