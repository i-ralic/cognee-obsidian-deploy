**Chat Canvas** lets you instantly turn your research into a clean, organized visual document. It automatically **structures your information**, so you can see the big picture without any manual work.

## How To Use AI Canvas

If you've finished gathering research within a **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|chat]]** and you want to transform it into a structured document, you can launch the Canvas view with one simple command:

**"Add research to Canvas and Open Canvas"**

This will analyze your entire research within that chat and open it in an organized way.

## Open Canvas

If you have already created your canvas and want to get back to it you can do it with a single click.

The **Canvas** button is located right next to where you select your **AI Agent**. It will open your previously created Canvas.

![](/media/1898141394/image.webp)

## Edit Canvas

The Canvas is designed to be a starting point, not the final word. You have **full control** to refine the AI's output.
​

To make a change, just click on any part of the text you want to adjust. A cursor will appear, allowing you to:

-   **Rewrite** sections that need a human touch

-   Expand or **remove** ideas based on your knowledge

-   **Add your own insights** or missing pieces

### Text Formating Toolbar

To help you polish that content into a final draft, we’ve included a full set of editing tools right at the top of your workspace. These tools help you emphasize important points and structure your writing:

-   **Bold, Italic, and Code**

-   **Headers**

-   **Dividers**

-   Bullet & Numbered Lists

-   Blockquotes
