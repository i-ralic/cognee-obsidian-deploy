When sharing a Cluing Cluster, you have the option to set **Access Roles** for users. This allows you to share Cluster with your colleagues and assign them permissions.

All you have to do is **enter their email** and assign the desired role to them.

Currently, supported permissions are:

-   **Viewer** - Can view snippets and comments for shared topic

-   **Commenter** - Can leave comments and reactions on snippets

-   **Editor** - Can add and remove snippets for a shared topic

-   **Owner** - The creator of a certain topic can edit Access Roles for that particular topic

![](/media/1619170468/image.webp)

## How to Add People to a Cluster via Email

-   **Go to Your Dashboard** - Open Cluing and navigate to your **Dashboard**.

-   **Open Cluster Settings** - Find the Cluster you want to share and click the **Settings** button next to it. A panel will open on the right.

![](/media/2513910083/image.webp)

-   **View Existing Members** – In the panel, you’ll see all members already added to the Cluster.

-   **Add New Members** – Enter the email address of the person you want to add.

-   **[[articles/11959751-what-different-roles-can-be-assigned-to-users|Assign Roles]]** - Choose the appropriate role for them:
    ​

    -   **Viewer** - Can view snippets and comments.

    -   **Commenter** - Can leave comments on snippets.

    -   **Editor** - Can add or remove snippets.

💡 If the person you invite doesn't have a Cluing account, we'll send them an email invitation to sign up. After creating an account, they'll be able to access the Cluster you've shared with them.

![](/media/2513915094/image.webp)

> [!warning]
> **_📌 Note: Ownership of a Cluster or Topic still can't be transferred to collaborators._**

## Accessing Shared Cluster

When someone shares a certain cluster with you, it will appear as a Cluster under the **“Shared with me”** section.
