In Cluing, you can easily export your data for sharing or storage.

## **Drag & Drop to another app**

If you want to export just part of your research:

-   Open the **Topic**.

-   Grab the snippet you want to export.

-   **Drag and drop** it directly into apps like **Google Docs, Notion, Outlook,** or **Gmail**.

<video src="/media/2208103672/gif---drag-and-drop.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1908" height="980" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

The snippet content will automatically be copied to the selected app.
