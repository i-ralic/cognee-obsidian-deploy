AI Credits are used whenever you interact with AI features in Cluing - things like chatting with the AI, organizing your workspace or getting AI-powered suggestions.

## Hobbyist

Free users have a monthly AI credit limit. You can always check your current balance in the **top right corner of the Dashboard**. Once your credits are used up, AI features will be unavailable until your credits refresh at the start of the next month.

![](/media/2405884514/image.webp)

## Professional **& Team Plans**

Professional and Team users have unlimited AI credits - no limits, no interruptions. Just use Cluing AI as much as you need.
