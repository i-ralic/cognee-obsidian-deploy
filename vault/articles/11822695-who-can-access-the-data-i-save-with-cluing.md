## **Personal Workspace**

The data on your personal plan is **completely private** until you choose otherwise!

A [[articles/11748259-what-is-a-topic|Topic]] or [[articles/11768730-what-is-a-cluster|Cluster]] you create is only visible to you by default, but you can choose to share it with additional people. You can set general permissions (for anyone receiving the link) or add individual user permissions.

If you are the one added to a topic, depending on the role you get assigned, you can view, comment, or edit the shared topics or clusters.

## Team Workspace

When using a team workspace, the created topics and clusters are **shared with the entire team** by default.

If there's sensitive data that should only be accessed by a select few, there's always the option of adding [[articles/11769020-private-clusters-team-version|a private cluster]].

Learn more about sharing options **[[articles/11792079-sharing-options|here.]] ⬅️**
