In addition to the **[[articles/15190588-dark-mode-on-your-dashboard|Dashboard]]**, Dark Mode is also available in the Cluing Extension 🌓

To change it, open the extension and click **Settings**. In the menu, find "**Appearance"**, where you can switch between Light and Dark mode.

This lets you adjust the look of the extension for a more comfortable viewing experience, especially in low-light environments.
