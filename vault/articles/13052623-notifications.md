Your notifications are represented as the **bell icon** located in the **top-left corner** of your Dashboard!
When you have unread updates, a **red bubble** will appear over the bell icon, showing you the exact number of new notifications you have.

![](/media/2514401939/image.webp)

​

### What You Get Notified About

You receive notifications for various activities, including:

-   **Topic/Snippet Activity**

-   **Snippet creation:** When someone creates a Snippet, changes the Snippet Title or adds a comment

-   **Direct Mention:** When someone mentions you using _[[articles/11793832-who-can-be-mentioned-in-the-comments|@mention]]_

> [!danger]
> **📍 Note:** You will **not** receive in-app notifications when you are added to a **Cluster**. Instead, these notifications are sent to you **via email**.

### How does it work

When you click on a notification:

1.  It instantly redirects you to the **exact location** the update represents (e.g., the specific comment or Snippet)

2.  If the notification is outdated (e.g., the Topic no longer exists or you were removed), we'll redirect you to your **HOME Page** instead

### Managing Notifications

If you want to stop receiving general activity updates for a particular Topic, you can always **[[articles/11767960-subscribe-or-unsubscribe-to-a-topic|unsubscribe]]** from it using the bell icon within that Topic.
