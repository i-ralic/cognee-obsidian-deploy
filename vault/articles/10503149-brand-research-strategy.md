When crafting a brand strategy or content plan, the first step is gaining _a deep understanding_ of the industry.

That usually includes researching the market, analyzing messaging and tone of voice, and spotting gaps and opportunities. But with so much information to sort through, keeping everything organized for further analysis can be overwhelming.

Here's how the [[articles/11839482-how-to-install-the-cluing-extension-and-create-your-first-topic|Cluing]] browser extension directly supports each phase of the process:

## **Capture & organize industry insights**

-   **Collected insights** - Highlight and save snippets from blogs, PDFs, newsletters, or social posts ([[articles/12638578-save-linkedin-posts-as-snippets|LinkedIn]], [[articles/12638616-save-reddit-posts-and-comments-as-snippets|Reddit]], [[articles/12638648-save-x-twitter-posts-as-snippets|X/Twitter]], [[articles/12638744-save-quora-posts-as-snippets|Quora]], Substack) to track how brands in your niche communicate, what topics they focus on, and which platforms they use - without switching tabs.

-   **On the go** - Use the Cluing [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US) app to save content while scrolling on mobile. Tap the native Share button from any app - LinkedIn, X, Substack, TikTok - and it lands directly in your Inbox.

-   **Summarize instead of reading** - Found a long competitor article or industry report? Use the [[articles/14211688-summarize-webpage|Summarize Webpage]] feature to let **AI extract** the key points and save them as a snippet automatically. No need to read everything word for word.

-   **Structured sources** - Sort saved snippets into Topics or Clusters to get a clear overview of the information you've collected.

-   **[[articles/11822856-what-s-a-clu-ing-link|Shareable links]]** - Get valuable input from industry experts with shareable links, so you never miss an important trend or insight.

### Break Down Messaging & Tone

-   **Personalized tags** - Label snippets based on tone (e.g., #casual, #authoritative, #playful) or platform (#email, #social, #ads) to identify patterns and gaps in messaging.

-   **Analyze competitor strategies** - Drag snippets from different sources into a single Topic to compare them side-by-side. This way you can spot inconsistencies or overused phrases.

-   **AI-powered analysis** - Once you've collected competitor messaging in a Topic, go to [[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]] and @mention the Topic directly in your prompt. Ask it to compare tones, identify recurring phrases, or surface what's missing. For example: _"Analyze @Competitor Messaging and tell me what tone patterns repeat across brands."_

-   **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|Direct mentions]]** - Mention collaborators in comments by typing "@" followed by their name or email. They'll get notified via email, or directly on Slack if connected.

### Find Gaps & Spot Opportunities

Great strategies aren't just about what's already being done — they find what's missing.

-   **Collaborative workspace** - Store your research in a shared workspace and invite clients, experts, or team members to [[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|collaborate]]. Assign roles (_View, Comment, Edit_) so everyone can contribute and refine insights.

-   **AI gap analysis** - After building up your research, [[articles/15444365-mention-a-topic-in-ai-chat|@mention your Topic]] in AI Chat and ask: _"What topics or angles are competitors not covering?"_ or _"Where are the messaging gaps in this niche?"_ The AI works directly with what you've saved, not generic training data.

-   **[[articles/15644219-export-options|Export & repurpose]]** - Export snippets as CSV or **PDF**, or simply drag & drop them into Google Docs, Notion, or Gmail - backed by real examples.

### Keep Everything Organized & Accessible

Instead of scrambling to relocate a key stat or competitor post, Cluing keeps everything connected. Every snippet is **linked to its source**, so you can always trace back your insights.

Think of it like **highlighting a book** but for the entire brand strategy. Every idea, source, and "aha!" moment stays connected, so you can focus on _creating_, not scrambling.

💡 **With all your insights in one place, you can confidently create a plan that's backed by data, easy to explain to clients, and simple to refine as trends evolve.**
