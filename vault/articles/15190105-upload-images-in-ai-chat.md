When you open **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]** on your Dashboard, you can upload images (_or other document types_) directly into the conversation.

Instead of describing what you're looking at, just **upload an image** or screenshot and let **Cluing AI analyze it for you**.

This makes it easier to get quick **answers**, **feedback**, **summaries**, or insights without any extra steps.
