Here’s how to remove workspace members:

-   Go to your **Dashboard**.

-   Click the **workspace name** in the top left corner.

-   At the bottom of the list, select **Settings and Members**.

-   From there, you’ll see the list of members and the option to **remove** them.

![](/media/2499636028/image.webp)

![](/media/2499639123/image.webp)

> [!danger]
> 📌 **NOTE:** To remove people from a workspace**,** you must be the **owner** of a **Team plan workspace**.
