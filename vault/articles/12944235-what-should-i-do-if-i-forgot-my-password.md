> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/14115869-security-and-privacy|Security and Privacy]]

If you forgot your Cluing password, don’t worry. 😊

On the login page, click **“Forgot password”** at the bottom.

![](/media/1850734569/image.webp)

A new page will pop up where you can reset your password. Simply enter your email, and you’ll receive a link to **set a new password and log in again**.

![](/media/1850736607/image.webp)
