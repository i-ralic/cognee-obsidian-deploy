Anything you open in Reader stays in your workspace - uploaded files and saved web pages alike. So when you need that report again next week, you don't have to re-upload it or hunt down the original link.

Here’s where you can find and manage your recently uploaded PDFs:

-   Go to your **Dashboard**

-   Open **Reader** from the left sidebar

-   In the top-left corner, click the **Document** icon

-   A list of all uploaded documents, including scraped webpages, will be displayed

-   From there, you can **open** or **delete** any document as needed.
