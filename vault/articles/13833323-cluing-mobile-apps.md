Cluing apps are the best place for saving social media posts, PDFs, and links while you're browsing on your phone.

## How to download the app on an Android phone

If you're using **Android**, you can go to the Play Store and type "Cluing" or [visit this link](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

![](/media/2095917225/Android-App.webp)

## How to download the app on an iPhone

If you're using **IOS**, you can go to the App Store and type "Cluing" or [visit this link](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072).

![](/media/2095927275/IOS-App.webp)
