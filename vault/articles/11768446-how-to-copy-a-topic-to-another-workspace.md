To copy a topic to another workspace:

1.  Open the Topic you want to copy.

2.  Click the three dots (_More Options_) in the top right corner.

3.  Select **Duplicate to**.

4.  Choose the workspace where you want to save the copy.

5.  Decide if you want to include **comments** or not.

![](/media/2513887352/image.webp)

This will create a full copy of the Topic (_with or without comments_) in the workspace you selected.
