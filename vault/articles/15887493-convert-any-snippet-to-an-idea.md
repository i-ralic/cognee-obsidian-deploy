With Convert to Idea, you can easily turn any snippet into an editable draft and continue building on your saved insights.

**Steps:**

1.  Open the snippet **menu** by clicking the three dots on the snippet

2.  Select **“Convert to Idea”**

3.  Your snippet will be automatically converted into an Idea, where you can **edit and expand** the content however you like
