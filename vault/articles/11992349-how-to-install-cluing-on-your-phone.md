## **Install Cluing on iOS**

1.  Open the **App Store**

2.  Search for **Cluing**

3.  Tap **Get** to install

## **Install Cluing on Android**

1.  Open the **Google Play Store**

2.  Search for **Cluing**

3.  Tap **Install**

Once installed, open the app, create a new account or log in with your existing one, and all your Topics and snippets will be right there waiting for you.
