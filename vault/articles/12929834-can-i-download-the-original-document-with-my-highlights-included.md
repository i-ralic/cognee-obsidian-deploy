At the moment, you **cannot download a Document** with your highlights applied to open in other apps.

However, you **can [[articles/11748989-print-or-save-a-topic-as-pdf|export a topic]]**, which will include all snippets and comments saved under that topic.

> [!note]
> **📌 Note:** PDF download with highlights is on our [roadmap](https://collabwriting.sleekplan.app/) and will be added in the near future.
