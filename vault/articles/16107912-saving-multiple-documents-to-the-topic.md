You can add multiple documents to a Topic at once and process them together, so all of their content becomes readable and searchable in a single click.
Once uploaded, each document is turned into a clean snippet - either as a summary or a simple bookmark - ready to read, highlight, and chat with.

There are two ways to add documents to a Topic:

## **From a new empty Topic**

1.  Go to your Dashboard and create a new Topic

2.  Select "Upload Documents"

## **Quick Add Button**

1.  Go to your Dashboard and open any Topic

2.  Click the Quick Add button in the top right corner

3.  Choose "Upload Documents" from the list

**From there** pick as many documents as you like and choose how you want to save them:

-   As a summarized snippet

-   As a bookmark snippet

After that just let Cluing handle the rest.

Cluing scans the document, pulls out the key points, and drops a clean snippet into your Topic.
