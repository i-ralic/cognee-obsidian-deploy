To delete a cluster, follow these steps:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**.

2.  Find the cluster you want to delete in the left sidebar.

3.  Click the **⚙️ settings button** next to the cluster name.

4.  A panel will open on the right side.

5.  At the bottom of the panel, click **Delete Cluster**.

![](/media/2469970831/image.webp)

![](/media/2469972995/image.webp)

> [!danger]
> ⚠️ **NOTE:** Deleting a cluster is permanent and cannot be undone.
