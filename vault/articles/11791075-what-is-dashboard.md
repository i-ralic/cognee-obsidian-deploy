The Dashboard is your home base in Cluing - where every Cluster, Topic, Snippet and research lives.

Cluing Dashboard is where you can:

-   Access and manage all your Topics and Clusters

-   [[articles/12701607-how-to-manage-a-cluing-workspace|Adjust workspace settings]]

-   Change your workspace name

-   Customize the style of your Shared Topics

-   Manage your [[articles/11791468-what-subscription-plans-are-available-in-cluing|payment plan]]

-   Update your user settings (first and last name, password)

-   Connect the [[articles/11776028-installing-the-cluing-slack-app|Slack workspace]]

-   Delete your account

You can access it either directly by visiting [app.cluing.io](https://app.cluing.io) or by clicking the Dashboard icon at the bottom of the Cluing Extension.

![](/media/2519127086/image.webp)

## How to access Dashboard from the Extension

-   Open the extension

-   Click the **Dashboard** button at the bottom

-   Your dashboard will open in a new browser tab
