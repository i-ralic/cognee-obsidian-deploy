> [[collections/14010526-subscriptions-plans-and-payments|Subscriptions, Plans and Payments]]

You can cancel your subscription at any time directly within your account.
​

### Here is how:

1.  Open your **Workspace Settings**

2.  Navigate to the **Subscription** section

3.  Click the **Manage Plan** button

4.  When new page is open - Click **Cancel Subscription** button

> [!tip]
> Your Pro features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

![](/media/2241220398/image.webp)

If for any reason this is not working, you can cancel your subscription at any time by sending a cancellation request via email to **[refunds@collabwriting.com](mailto:refunds@collabwriting.com)** from your registered email address.

In your email, please include:

-   A brief reason why you want to cancel

-   Your billing information (name, transaction ID, last 4 digits of the credit card, billing date, etc.)

-   Your email address and login name

-   Any other info needed for identity verification

**Read more here: [Cluing Refund Policy](https://cluing.io/legal/refund-policy) 👈🏼**
