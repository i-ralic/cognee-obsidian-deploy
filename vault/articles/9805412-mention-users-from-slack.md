Once you [[articles/11776028-installing-the-cluing-slack-app|connect your Slack]] workspace to your Cluing account, you'll be able to directly mention your Slack teammates within the Cluing mention popup.

Instead of checking notifications in Cluing or digging through email to find a shared snippet, your teammate gets a **direct message in your team's Slack**.
​

### To do so, follow these steps:

1.  Type @ inside of the comment field

2.  Wait for the list of potential mentions to appear

3.  Scroll up to find the "Slack Members" section

4.  Choose the Slack teammate you want to mention from the list

## Mention only

**Only Mention** lets you mention people from your Slack workspace **without sharing the entire Topic** with them.

They'll receive a notification in their **Slack inbox** with a snippet of content, any comment you attached, and an option to locate it.
