Think of your AI chat as a dynamic workspace where you get to choose exactly how you want to tackle a task.

Depending on what you're working on, you can toggle between two types of AI assistance:

## **Models**

Gives you direct access to standard frontier engines (like Claude, GPT, or Gemini) for pure, open-ended brainpower without specialized constraints.

## **Agents**

Pre-configured digital assistants equipped with custom tools and domain expertise designed to jump straight into specific roles.

> [!tip]
> 📍 **Tip**: [[articles/15800127-ai-agents-and-pro-ai-models-in-cluing-chat|Toggle between them]] at any time using the selector icon inside your chat bar to find the perfect fit for your current task.

## Free Users

As a free user, you have access to all free AI models on the market at the moment. Also, some agents are available too.
