You can turn any email into a structured snippet within your workspace.
By assigning a unique email address to a specific Topic, you can forward any email directly into your Topic.
​

## How to set it up?

1.  Open workspace settings

2.  Navigate to "Emails" section

3.  Click to add a new email

4.  Choose Topic

5.  The system will generate a unique address

> [!tip]
> **Pro/Team Users:** You have the [[articles/14354712-custom-domain-and-email-address-setup-pro-team|option to customize your address]] and domain for a more professional touch.

![](/media/2253100304/Emails.webp)

## How it works?

-   Send or [[articles/14058819-organize-your-newsletters-with-cluing|forward any email]] to that unique address

-   The app will automatically process the email and create a new snippet within the chosen Topic

-   Simply use the Locate button to view the full content in the Reader Page

> [!warning]
> **Note:** The number of email integrations available depends on your plan. Users on the **Free** plan are limited to **1** active email address, while the **Personal Pro** and **Team version** allows you to create **multiple** email addresses for different Topics.
