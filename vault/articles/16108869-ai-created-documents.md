AI Chat can write a full document straight into your workspace.

Ask for a report, a guide, a write-up, or anything else you need in long form - Cluing puts it together and saves it as a Document so you can open and read anytime.

## **How to create one**

1.  Open AI Chat from your Dashboard

2.  Ask the AI to write the document you need - for example: _"Write me a competitive analysis report on Twitter"_

3.  The AI generates the document and saves it in your Reader

4.  Open it in **Reader** to read the full text

> [!warning]
> 📌 AI-created documents behave like any other Document, so everything you can do in Reader works here too.

## What you can do next

-   **Highlight** any passage to turn it into a snippet

-   **Comment and tag** for collaboration, just like any other Reader content

-   **Reference it later in AI Chat** — ask Cluing to pull the document back into a new conversation, expand a section, or use it as the starting point for something new

## **A few ideas to get started**

-   A summary report on a topic you've been researching

-   A structured guide or how-to for your team

-   A draft write-up based on notes saved in your Topics
