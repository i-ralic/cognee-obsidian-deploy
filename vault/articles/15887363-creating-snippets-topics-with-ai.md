Cluing AI can now create and organize content directly in your workspace.

Whether you’re chatting with **Cluing AI** or using Claude through the **[[articles/15178437-connecting-claude-to-cluing-via-mcp|Cluing MCP]]** integration, you can turn conversations and insights into Snippets, Ideas, and Topics without manually creating them yourself.

## **What you can create**

Depending on what you ask for, the AI can generate:

-   **Summary snippet** - turns the conversation or a part of it into a concise summary

-   **Bookmark snippet** - saves a specific piece of content for quick reference later

-   **Idea snippet** - captures a thought or insight worth revisiting and refining later

-   **Topic** - the AI groups related snippets into a corresponding Topic

## **How to use it**

Creating a snippet or Topic is as simple as asking for one in chat.

1.  Have a conversation with the AI as you normally would.

2.  Ask the AI to create a snippet - for example:

    -   "Summarize this conversation into a snippet"

    -   "Bookmark this for later"

    -   "Turn this into IDEA snippet"

> [!warning]
> 📌 You can also ask for specific content within a snippet - for example:
>
> _"Create an idea snippet about the pricing options we discussed."_

##
Create Snippets, Ideas, and Topics in Cluing with Claude MCP

With the Cluing **[[articles/15393497-what-is-mcp-and-why-does-it-matter|MCP integration]]**, Claude can now create content directly in your Cluing workspace.

You can ask Claude to create:

-   **Summary Snippets** - turn a conversation or selected content into a concise summary.

-   **Bookmark Snippets** - save important information for quick reference later.

-   **Idea Snippets** - capture thoughts and insights you want to develop further.

-   **Topics** - organize related snippets into a dedicated Topic.

Your workspace updates automatically as Claude creates new content in Cluing.
