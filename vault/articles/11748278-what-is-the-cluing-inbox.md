**Inbox is your "everything" space** - a place to save anything important before you decide how and where to organize it.

Unlike Topics, Inbox is a completely **private space**, even for team users, where all unfiltered thoughts and notes can be stored.

> [!warning]
> 📌 _In the extension settings, you can choose to have the **[[articles/12638164-open-on-startup#:~:text=and%20save%20time. ,Open%20Inbox%20on%20Startup, If%20you%20want|Inbox open by default]]** when you launch the extension._

<video src="/media/2537934543/InboxNewExtHighlight.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1888" height="906" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Once you're ready to organize your saved snippets, go to your Dashboard. Here you’ll find your Inbox at the top left of the side menu.

From there, you can easily **drag and drop** snippets into the right Topics.

<video src="/media/2513873070/inbox-sort-further.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="986" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

> [!danger]
> 📌 **NOTE:** You can’t add collaborators to your Inbox, but you _can_ [[articles/11830845-mention-only|mention someone]] by their email under a snippet if you need their input. They’ll receive that snippet via email and will be able to **locate it** on the original source, but they won’t see the rest of your Inbox.
