If you want to copy content from just one snippet, here’s how:

-   Find the snippet (in the **Dashboard** or the **Extension**)

-   Click on the **Snippet Menu Options** (_three dots_)

-   From the list, select **Copy Content**

![](/media/2470570706/image.webp)

Now you can **paste the content into any text editor** or document where you need it.
