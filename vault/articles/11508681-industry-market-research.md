## Making Sense of Market Research

When preparing industry or market research, the hard part isn't reading. It's **sorting signal from noise**, tracking sources, and keeping everything structured enough to make sense later.

You're juggling analyst reports, whitepapers, regulatory updates, competitor blogs, LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos - all while preparing something **clear for your client**.

And usually you end up with 27 reports, 13 YouTube videos, 43 open tabs, 8 half-written notes in Slack, and a spreadsheet no one wants to open.

That's why the [[articles/11839482-how-to-install-the-cluing-extension-and-create-your-first-topic|Cluing Extension]] is so helpful. Save important insights as you read, keep them neatly organized, and find exactly what you need - without the chaos.

## Cluing makes it easier

Instead of manually saving links or copying quotes and data into Notion or Google Docs, use Cluing to highlight what matters _as you read_ - **from any source**.

-   Articles and reports (PDFs or web)

-   Reddit threads and LinkedIn updates

-   YouTube videos

-   Market data platforms

Just highlight, comment, add a hashtag - and move on.

Every highlight you make is saved with:

-   **The original link**

-   **Your comment -** the context only you can add

-   **The source context** (so you don’t have to read the whole thing again)

## Structure by client, project or theme

Your workspace can mirror how your team works, which means you can organize research by:

-   **[[articles/11748259-what-is-a-topic|Topics]]** - focused boards for each research angle (e.g., ’’AI in Healthcare’’, ’’Competitor X’’, or ’’Market Sizing’’)

-   ​**[[articles/11768730-what-is-a-cluster|Clusters]]** \- group related Topics under a bigger project or client. For example: ’’Sustainable Energy Market’’ → includes Topics like ’’Solar Technologies’’, ’’Government Policies’’, ’’Key Competitors’’ and ’’Market Growth Projections’’.

-   ​**[[articles/11792004-permission-levels-explained|Permissions]]** - decide who can view, comment, or edit (perfect for junior researchers, external partners, or clients)

## Make collaboration effortless

Whether you're working with teammates, clients, or external experts, Cluing keeps everyone aligned around the same context.

There are **multiple ways to collaborate**, depending on what you want to share:

-   **[[articles/11793739-mention-external-users-by-email|Invite collaborators]]** to a Topic or Cluster with **View, Comment, or Edit** permissions.

-   **[[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|Share a clu.ing link]]** when you only want to send a few selected Snippets without exposing your entire workspace.

-   **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|@Mention teammates]]** directly on a Snippet or comment to bring the right people into the discussion. They'll receive a notification via Slack or email and can jump straight to the relevant insight.

-   **[[articles/11830845-mention-only|Leave comments]]** on individual Snippets to discuss findings exactly where they matter, instead of across long email threads.

-   **[[articles/11793696-mention-collaborators-from-slack|Collaborate in Slack]]** with notifications for mentions and comments that link directly back to the relevant insight.

Research is expensive. Repeating it is even worse. But with Cluing, your findings **don’t disappear into old threads or docs**.

Instead:

-   You build a living **knowledge base**

-   You find old insights fast (thanks to **AI search** & **tags**)

-   You can reuse research across teams and future projects

-   Your next research sprint is 10× faster

### Go deeper with AI Chat

Once your Topics are populated, open **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]** and **[[articles/15444365-mention-a-topic-in-ai-chat|mention a Topic]]** to bring your research into the conversation.

AI Chat doesn't browse the internet. It works with **your snippets and the context you've added to them**. The more detailed your comments and notes, the more useful the output.

Ask things like:

-   _"What are the 3 biggest market shifts across @SolarTechnologies?"_

-   _"Summarize competitive positioning from @KeyCompetitors"_

-   _"What's still missing from @MarketGrowthProjections?"_

Mention **multiple Topics** at once - AI will connect insights across workstreams and surface patterns you might have missed.

Found something useful in the conversation? **[[articles/14019248-save-snippet-from-ai-chat|Save it as a Snippet]]** directly from AI Chat, right into the relevant Topic.

### Upload reports and client documents

When a client sends you background materials - a strategy deck, an internal report, a previous market study - **[[articles/15442354-upload-documents-to-ai-chat|upload them directly into AI Chat]]**.

No need to read the whole thing first. Just upload and ask:

-   _"What are the main findings of this report?"_

-   _"What does this say about market share trends?"_

-   _"Compare this with what I already have in @MarketOverview"_

Save the key AI-extracted points back into your Topic as Snippets, so they sit alongside your other research, properly sourced.

### Draft client deliverables in Canvas

When the research is done, you still need to **write the output**. Open **[[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|Canvas]]** from AI Chat and let it draft, with your Topics as context.

Ask it to:

-   _"Write an executive summary based on @MarketOverview and @KeyCompetitors"_

-   _"Draft a recommendations section based on the gaps we identified"_

-   _"Create a structured briefing from @SustainableEnergyMarket"_

Canvas opens with the draft ready to edit - restructure sections, add your own analysis, format it for the client. Then **export or share** when it's ready.
