Reviewing contracts is rarely a solo task. Whether you're updating clauses, aligning on legal language, or checking compliance requirements, multiple stakeholders are usually involved - legal, finance, procurement, operations, and sometimes external counsel.

The challenge is not only reviewing the document itself. It's keeping track of **who said what, why a decision was made, and what context led to the final agreement**.

Contract feedback often gets scattered across email threads, tracked changes, Slack conversations, and **multiple versions** of the same document. Important insights disappear, and teams lose the reasoning behind previous decisions.

> [!warning]
> _📌 Tools like Google Docs or spreadsheets can help with collaboration, but they rarely preserve the full picture. The most valuable knowledge is often hidden across PDFs, emails, legal websites, previous agreements, and conversations between stakeholders._

Cluing gives your team **one shared workspace** to capture, organize, and discuss knowledge, turning individual observations into **shared, reusable insights**.

Every highlight, comment, and decision stays **connected to its original source**, giving your team and **AI the context** needed to work smarter.

## Keep your team in sync

Ever needed to share just a few important sentences from a 100-page contract but ended up sending the entire PDF with a message like:

_"Page 15, second paragraph under the table..."_

With Cluing, you can **highlight exactly what matters**, leave comments directly on each clause, and keep every discussion attached to the original source.

Getting started is simple:

-   Click the Cluing browser extension.

-   Open your **[[articles/15442545-document-management-in-reader|PDF in Reader]]**.

-   Highlight important clauses and leave comments as you review.

Each highlight becomes a **Snippet** that's permanently linked to its exact location in the original document.

Instead of collecting disconnected notes, your team builds a library of **contextual insights**. Every comment, discussion, and decision stays attached to the clause it refers to, preserving not only what matters, but **why** it matters.

You can also combine snippets from online **PDFs**, uploaded documents, websites, and **[[articles/11784650-capture-youtube-videos|YouTube videos]]** into a single Topic, keeping all related insights connected and easy to revisit.

## Analyze contracts with AI using your team's context

Reading a contract from beginning to end isn't always the best place to start.

**[[articles/15442354-upload-documents-to-ai-chat|Upload]]** your contract directly into **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]** and let Cluing help identify what matters before your team begins reviewing.

Ask questions like:

-   _What are the biggest legal risks in this agreement?_

-   _Summarize each party's obligations._

-   _List all payment deadlines and notice periods._

-   _Are there any unusual indemnity or limitation-of-liability clauses?_

-   _Which sections should legal review before signing?_

Cluing AI analyzes the document and responds based on its content.

You can bring **additional context** directly into your conversation by **[[articles/15444365-mention-a-topic-in-ai-chat|mentioning]]** a specific Topic that contains your team's collected insights, previous contract reviews, and related research.

You can also upload additional **documents**, **images**, or screenshots to help AI understand the full picture.

> [!note]
> 📌 _For example, you can mention a Topic containing previous vendor agreements, upload a related policy document, and ask AI to compare terms, identify differences, or highlight potential risks._

Once you've identified important sections, you can turn those insights into **[[articles/14019248-save-snippet-from-ai-chat|Snippets directly from AI Chat]]**. Ask Cluing to save key findings, summaries, or recommendations into [[articles/15887363-creating-snippets-topics-with-ai|a specific Topic]], and they’ll be stored alongside your existing research with the right context.

From there, you can add your team's notes, @mention relevant stakeholders, and continue **collaborating around the exact insights** that matter.

## Bring the right people into the right clauses

Not everyone involved in a contract needs to review every page.

Once you've highlighted the important clauses, simply **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|@mention]]** the right teammate directly on the relevant Snippet.

For example:

-   **Legal reviews** indemnity and liability clauses.

-   **Finance verifies** payment schedules.

-   **Procurement checks** vendor obligations.

-   **Operations reviews** delivery timelines and SLAs.

Each teammate receives a **notification** and lands directly on the highlighted clause -instead of searching through a PDF or digging through email threads.

Everyone sees the same source, the same discussion, and the same context behind the decision.

If your team uses [[articles/11793696-mention-collaborators-from-slack|Slack]], mentions can be delivered there too, keeping conversations connected to the original contract.

Need to share only a few important findings with someone outside your workspace?

Simply send them a **[[articles/11792146-sharing-topics-with-clu-ing-links|clu.ing]]** link containing your selected Snippets. If they need additional context, they can jump directly to the original location inside the document.

## Organize every contract and discussion

As your contract library grows, keeping information organized becomes just as important as reviewing it.

Cluing helps structure your team's knowledge so every insight remains easy to find and reuse.

-   **[[articles/11785748-what-is-a-workspace-in-cluing|Workspaces]]** give your entire legal team a shared space to manage insights across ongoing matters or practice areas.

-   **[[articles/11768730-what-is-a-cluster|Clusters]]** let you group Topics by case, regulation, client, or jurisdiction. Assign permissions at the Cluster level to keep sensitive work properly scoped while allowing the right people to collaborate.

-   **[[articles/11748259-what-is-a-topic|Topics]]** are where you save specific highlights from legal documents, rulings, and webpages.Invite colleagues, clients, or external counsel with View, Comment, or Edit [[articles/11792004-permission-levels-explained|permissions]] depending on their role.

-   With **[[articles/11783020-searching-by-tags|#tags]]**, you can easily categorize and retrieve key insights, whether it's #GDPR, #IP, or #compliance. Add them directly to a snippet or include them in a comment - they’ll be searchable either way.

## Make knowledge reusable

Every contract review creates unique insights.

It captures more than the contract language itself - it captures _why_ a clause was flagged, what risks were identified, what decisions were made, and how your team approached the negotiation.

Instead of letting that knowledge disappear inside email threads or meeting notes, Cluing preserves it with its original context.

Create dedicated Topics for:

-   **_Vendor Agreements_**

-   **_NDAs_**

-   **_Data Processing Agreements_**

-   **_Employment Contracts_**

-   **_GDPR Requirements_**

-   **_Procurement Policies_**

Over time, your workspace becomes more than a collection of contracts. It becomes a **searchable knowledge base** of your organization's legal reasoning, negotiation history, and best practices.

When someone asks:

_"What does our standard NDA say about data retention?"_

or

_"Have we accepted this liability clause before?"_

Open **AI Chat**, mention the relevant Topic, and let Cluing search across your saved knowledge to provide an answer based on your **team's existing context**.

> [!note]
> **💡 Tip:** Need to reference relevant US case law during contract review? Use [Cluing's Caselaw Search](https://www.cluing.io/caselaw) to search through a database of nearly **7 million cases** - then save key findings as snippets directly into your Topic.

## Create executive summaries with AI

Not every stakeholder wants (or needs) to read an entire contract.

After your review is complete, use AI Chat to generate:

-   executive summaries

-   legal risk briefings

-   negotiation summaries

-   key obligations for each party

-   action items for internal teams

Mention your contract Topic and ask Cluing to **draft the documen**t you need.

For example:

_"Create a one-page summary of the key terms and obligations for the executive team."_

or

_"Draft a risk briefing based on the clauses we've flagged."_

Open the response in **[[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|Canvas]]**, where you can continue editing with AI, refine the wording, reorganize sections, and prepare the final document for sharing.

Because AI has access to both the contract and your team's saved context, the output reflects not only what's written in the document, but also the **insights your organization has built** over time.

## Export and share your work

Once your review is complete, Cluing makes it easy to share your work however your team prefers.

-   **[[articles/11749010-how-to-make-a-copy-of-a-topic|Copy Topic]]** creates a complete duplicate of a Topic - including comments if you'd like - even across different workspaces.

-   **[[articles/11775995-copy-all-your-snippets-with-comments|Copy Snippets]]** copies selected highlights directly into Google Docs, Microsoft Word, Notion, or any editor you use.

-   **[[articles/11768139-download-topic-as-a-csv-file|Download CSV]]** exports all Snippets from a Topic into a spreadsheet for reporting or further analysis.

-   **[[articles/11748989-print-or-save-a-topic-as-pdf|Print]]** generates a printable PDF containing your Snippets and comments.

-   **[[articles/11775739-drag-drop-snippets-to-external-sources|Drag & Drop]]** lets you move Snippets directly into Gmail, Notion, Google Docs, or other applications without extra formatting.

## Work better together - with context that compounds over time

Contracts contain valuable information, but the real value comes from preserving the context around them.

Cluing helps teams capture their unique insights, connect them to the original source, and make them **reusable across future agreements**.

As your workspace grows, AI has richer context to work with - helping legal, finance, procurement, and operations teams review contracts faster, make better decisions, and **build on knowledge instead** of starting from scratch.
