The **Knowledge Triggers** ensure you never miss a connection between your current research and content you've previously saved in Cluing.

It eliminates the guesswork of "_Did I save this before?_" 🤔

> [!warning]
> **Note**: To enable this functionality, you must first turn ON "**[[articles/11822407-adjusting-extension-preferences#h_b3000cb173|Experimental features]]"** within your Extension settings!

## **How Knowledge Triggers Work**

When you visit any webpage, Cluing automatically checks your entire library of saved content.

-   **Instant Visual Cues:** Any text on the current page that you have previously highlighted and saved as a snippet will now appear **subtly underlined** right on the page.
    ​

-   **Locate the Source:** To instantly find the saved context for that underlined text, simply **click on the underlined text**. You will be immediately redirected to the exact snippet and Topic, showing you where and why you saved it.
