You can upload **multiple documents** (_like PDFs, CSVs, and [[articles/15190105-upload-images-in-ai-chat|Images]]_) into AI Chat in Cluing. Just drop in a file, ask a question, and get instant answers - without leaving the conversation.

![](/media/2532461636/image.webp)

## **How to upload a file**

1.  Open the AI Chat

2.  Click the **upload button** on the right side of the input field

3.  Select one of the files from your device (PDF or other supported document types)

4.  Type your message and send

<video src="/media/2466987530/AnalyzeDocumentWithAI.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="906" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

Once uploaded, you can **ask the AI anything about the file** - summarize it, extract key points, compare documents, or ask specific questions about the content.

> [!warning]
> **📌 You can even ask the AI to find the precise paragraph in your documents, understanding meaning rather than matching keywords.**

The AI will use the file as context to give you more accurate and relevant responses.
