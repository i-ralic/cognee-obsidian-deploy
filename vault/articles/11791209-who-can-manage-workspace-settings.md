> [[collections/13994794-workspace-and-account-settings|Workspace and Account Settings]]

> [!danger]
> 📌 Only **workspace owners** can access settings and invite members.

Which means that if you're in a **team workspace** but you're **not the owner**, you won’t have access to the **settings** or the option to **invite members**.
