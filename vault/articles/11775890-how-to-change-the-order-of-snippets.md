With **Drag & Drop**, you can quickly reorder snippets to keep your information organized and easily accessible for both you and your team.

Here's how:

## On the Dashboard

1.  Choose the topic where you want to reorder the snippets.

2.  Click and drag a snippet to the desired position, then release it.

<video src="/media/2519078527/change-the-order-of-the-snippets.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="986" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

## In the Extension

1.  **Open the extension** and choose the topic where you want to reorder the snippets.

2.  **Click and drag** a snippet to the desired position, then release it.
