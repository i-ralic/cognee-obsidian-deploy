### **Writing a Blog Takes More Than Just Writing**

Before a single word hits the page, there's an entire process most people don't see.

It starts with **research**: scanning competitor blogs, LinkedIn threads, YouTube videos, and newsletters to understand what's already been said - and what's still missing.

Then comes the **collecting phase**: bookmarking articles, screenshotting stats, copying quotes, saving PDFs. Before long, you have 30 open tabs, highlights scattered across three apps, and notes living in four different places.

Then you have to make sense of it all.

_What's actually relevant? What fits the angle you're going for? How should the post be structured?_

That quiet, invisible organizing can swallow an hour before you've written a single sentence. And that's _before_ the drafting, the edits, the fact-checking, or the endless back-and-forth if you're working with a team.

So by the time you finally sit down to write, you're already spent. The morning is gone, the research is a mess, and the blank page is still sitting there, waiting.

## AI Chat + Canvas: One Place for the Whole Workflow

Cluing's **[AI Chat and Canvas](https://www.cluing.io/canvas/)** were built for exactly this moment. The idea is simple: turn the hours you lose organizing research into minutes, so writing stops feeling like assembly and starts feeling like craft again.

Here's what that looks like in practice.

### **Research without the tab chaos**

Start by collecting the best ideas wherever you find them. Save articles, [[articles/11784694-capture-content-from-social-media-on-desktop|social posts]], [[articles/11784650-capture-youtube-videos|videos]], [[articles/11784127-how-to-upload-pdfs-to-your-workspace|PDFs]], [[articles/14058819-organize-your-newsletters-with-cluing|newsletters]], and other resources directly into Cluing, building a research library that grows alongside your project.

Then ask a **[[articles/15800127-ai-agents-and-pro-ai-models-in-cluing-chat|Pro AI Model]] - from OpenAI, Anthropic, Grok, or DeepSeek** - to hunt down a stat, summarize a source, or **surface an insight** buried in your saved snippets, and it lands right where you're working.

![](/media/2542138238/image.webp)

And when you want more than a raw model, you can hand the work to a **Custom AI Agent** built for the job.

Lean on the **🧐 Researcher** to gather and weigh sources, the **✍🏼 Creative Writer** to shape a rough idea into clean copy, the **📢 Marketing Expert** to sharpen your messaging, or the **📰 Press Release Writer** when it's time to go public.

You can **switch between agents and models** mid-conversation, so the assistant always matches the task in front of you - never the other way around.

### AI that knows what you already know

Most AI tools start from a blank slate every time you open them, which means you spend the first ten minutes just re-explaining yourself.

Cluing works the other way around. It **draws on the research library you've already built** - your notes, your highlights, your uploaded documents - so its answers are grounded in the work you've already done.

You can go a step further and **[[articles/15444365-mention-a-topic-in-ai-chat|mention a Topic]]** right in your prompt. Type **@**, pick a Topic, and Cluing feeds everything saved inside it to the **AI as context**.

Suddenly you can ask it to spot the trends across a month of research, summarize an entire body of notes, or answer a pointed question against your own knowledge base, and get back something that actually sounds like _your_ material, not the internet's average take.

Better still, you can **drop your files straight** into the chat.

**[[articles/15442354-upload-documents-to-ai-chat|Upload]]** **several documents at once** - PDFs, CSVs, even [[articles/15190105-upload-images-in-ai-chat|screenshots]] - and ask questions across all of them together.

Compare two reports, pull the key numbers out of a study, or find the exact paragraph you half-remember. Because the AI reads for meaning rather than matching keywords, it surfaces the passage you need instead of the one that merely shares a word with your search.

### From scattered notes to a shape you can write into

Once the insights are in, the **[[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|AI Canvas]]** turns them into a structured, visual document you can actually see. The flow of your post appears in front of you, sections and all, ready to be rearranged, tightened, or reworked. It's less a blank page and more a **ready-to-draft outline** - the scaffolding already in place before you write your first real line.

Cluing AI can **[[articles/15887363-creating-snippets-topics-with-ai|create and organize content for you]]** - turning a conversation into a Summary, Bookmark, or Idea snippet, or grouping loose snippets into a brand-new Topic without you saving a thing by hand.

### Write, edit, and make it yours - right there

From the Canvas, you slide straight into drafting.

Reshape the AI's suggestions, move sections around, and **layer in your own voice** and expertise. Nothing gets copied between apps, nothing gets lost in the handoff - the entire journey, from raw research to finished draft, happens in a single workspace.

And because **every answer carries its source links**, you always know exactly where a claim came from. 🔗

When research, organization, drafting, and editing finally share one connected space, the whole process gets lighter:

-   **You can trust your sources** - every AI-generated insight links back to where it came from.
