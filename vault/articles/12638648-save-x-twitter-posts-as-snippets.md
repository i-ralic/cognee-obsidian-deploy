**Open the Extension**

-   To save posts from Twitter, all you have to do is go to the Twitter app and **open the extension** in your browser.

**Navigate to the Save Button**

-   You'll find a "**Save**" button located beneath each post on your Twitter home page.

-   Clicking this button automatically captures the post's description and stores it **as a snippet** within the extension, making it easy to revisit later.

![](/media/2003340979/GBdISmfurN.webp)

## How to save an X post or news from your phone to Cluing

First, install the Cluing mobile app - available for both [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

1.  Find the X post or news/article you want to save.

2.  Tap the **Share** button.

3.  Select **Cluing** from the share options.

4.  Choose the **Topic** where you want to save it.

5.  Tap **Create**.

That’s it - quick and simple.

Your saved content is always **easy to find** in your workspace, and even if the original creator deletes it, your copy stays **safely stored in Cluing**.
