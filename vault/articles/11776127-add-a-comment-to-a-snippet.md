> [!note] In this collection
> [[collections/13970558-snippets-comments-tags|Snippets, Comments, Tags]] / [[collections/13973019-comments|Comments]]

You’ll find the **comment box** right below every snippet, whether you're on the **Dashboard** or using the **browser extension**.

Use it to add your thoughts, context, or reminders, so both others and your future self understand why the snippet matters.

![](/media/1810416476/image.webp)

📌 Comments aren’t just for notes. You can also **mention people** in different ways:

-   **[[articles/11830845-mention-only|Only Mention]]** - Just mention someone’s name to notify them and bring them into the conversation.

-   **[[articles/11959751-what-different-roles-can-be-assigned-to-users|Mention + Role Assignment]]** - When mentioning someone, you can also assign them a role (**Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**) to control what they can do within that topic.

-   **[[articles/11776096-mentioning-users-from-slack|Slack Mention]]** - You can mention someone directly on **Slack**, and they’ll get notified there.
