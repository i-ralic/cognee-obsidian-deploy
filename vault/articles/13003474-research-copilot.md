With Research Copilot, starting your research and saving insights has never been easier. Here’s how it works:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Click the **\+ button** on a cluster to create a **[[articles/11748302-how-to-create-a-new-topic|blank topic]]**

3.  Once the topic is created, click "**Copilot Research**"

![](/media/2511351780/image.webp)

Research Copilot will search the web for you, and when it’s done, **relevant snippets are automatically saved** directly within your topic.

> [!warning]
> 📌 All snippets collected by Research Copilot are **organized directly in your topic**, ready for you to comment, tag, or collaborate with your team.

You can also start your research in other ways:

-   Open a **[[articles/12267724-cluing-reader|PDF or webpage]]** by pasting a link

-   Create Idea

-   Save a link as a **[[articles/11783251-bookmarks-save-now-read-later|bookmark]]**

-   **[[articles/15191237-how-to-summarize-links-from-an-empty-topic|Summarize]]** link

-   **Search the web** manually with Copilot
