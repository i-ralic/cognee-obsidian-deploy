You can now capture screenshots from Youtube videos and locate that exact timestamp later.

## Open the Extension

To capture a screenshot from YouTube, simply open the YouTube video and launch the Cluing extension in your browser.

## Navigate to the Save button

You'll find a "**Save**" button located beneath each video. Clicking this button automatically captures the screenshot and stores it **as a snippet** within the extension, making it easy to revisit later.

## Preview Video

On the dashboard, you can preview any saved video.

Just open the Topic, hover over the snippet, and a **play button** will appear. This lets you watch a **15-second preview** starting from the exact timestamp you saved.
