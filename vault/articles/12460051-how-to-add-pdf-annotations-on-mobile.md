Making notes and highlighting key parts of your PDFs is super easy and intuitive even on mobile.

Here are the quick steps:

### **Open Cluing**

-   Download the Cluing app - available for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cluing/id6759099072) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.cluing.apps&hl=en_US).

### **Go to Reader**

-   Tap the **Reader icon** in the bottom navigation bar. This is where all your uploaded PDFs appear.

### **Open a PDF** and choose one of the options:

-   **Upload a PDF** from your device, or

-   Paste a link to a **public PDF**

![](/media/2499806822/image.webp)

### **How to Create Snippets on a PDF**

-   Highlight any text on the PDF

-   A **highlighter button** will appear next to your highlighted selection

From there, you can:

-   **Choose a Topic and a Cluster** where you want to save the snippet

-   Add a comment, **tag,** or **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|mention]]** someone

-   Confirm and save, all in a few clicks

![](/media/2499811724/image.webp)

> [!note]
> **🤖 [[articles/11928105-highlighter-button|AI Highlighter Suggestions]]**
> ​
> When you highlight text and click "Save", you’ll see **AI-powered suggestions** for: Topic (Cluster) Title, Tags
>
> These suggestions are based on your previous research activity, making it faster and easier to **categorize what you save**.
