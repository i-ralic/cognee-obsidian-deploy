If you save a PDF or Canvas document as a snippet in a public Topic, anyone with the link can open and **read it in view-only mode** - no Cluing account required.

This works for two types of Reader snippets:

-   **PDF snippets** - PDFs you've opened and saved directly into a Topic

-   **Canvas snippets** - Canvas documents you've saved back into a Topic after drafting or editing

## How to share a Reader snippet publicly

**1\. Save your content as a snippet**

-   For a **PDF**: open the PDF in Cluing and save it into a Topic

-   For a **Canvas**: finish working in Canvas, then save it as a snippet back into a Topic

**2\. Set the Topic to public**

-   Open the Topic, go to **General Access**, and set it to **"Anyone with the link"**

**3\. Share the clu.ing link**

-   Copy the Topic link and send it to whoever needs access

👉🏼 When the recipient opens the link, they click **Locate** next to the snippet - this opens the content in the **Reader in view-only mode**. No sign-up, no account needed.
