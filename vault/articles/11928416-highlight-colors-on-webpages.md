🎨 You can now choose different colors for your highlights directly on any webpage.

## How to change the highlight color:

1.  Highlight text

2.  Save it as a Snippet

3.  Click on the **color palette icon** that appears after saving

4.  Choose a color from the dropdown menu

Your selected color will be saved along with the snippet.
