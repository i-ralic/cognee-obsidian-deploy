You can easily organize your saved content, keeping your research exactly where you need it. There are 2 ways of doing it

## **To move a snippet to another topic:**

### Drag & Drop

1.  **Click and hold** the snippet.

2.  **Drag** it to the desired topic.

3.  **Release** to drop it.

<video src="/media/1936581473/move-snippet-.mp4" aria-hidden="true" width="1910" height="986" autoplay loop muted playsinline preload="metadata" ></video>

### "Move To" Option

1.  Open the Snippet Menu

2.  Select "Move To" option

3.  Select a Cluster

4.  Choose a Topic for the Snippet

That’s it! Your snippet has been moved.
