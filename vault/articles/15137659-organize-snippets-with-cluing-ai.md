Managing a messy workspace can be frustrating.

Instead of spending time manually moving snippets between Topics and reorganizing your knowledge, let our **specialized AI agent** keep everything structured for you.

The **Workspace Organizer** is a dedicated AI agent built specifically to sort, group, and structure your research instantly.

![](/media/2532531375/image.webp)

## How to organize Snippets using Chat

1.  Open the **AI Chat** inside your workspace

2.  **Select the Workspace Organizer** agent from the agent list

3.  Tell the Agent exactly how you want to organize your snippets

4.  The AI will **analyze your snippets** and show a list of proposed changes

5.  If you disagree with a suggestion or want to keep a snippet where it is, simply uncheck the box next to that snippet

6.  Click **Apply** to execute the moves you've approved

Once you hit Apply, the AI takes care of the rest and moves snippets to their new Topics.
