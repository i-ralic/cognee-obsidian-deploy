Every day, social media teams juggle endless content - viral posts, competitor updates, trending hashtags, eye-catching visuals, and influencer insights.

Traditional methods of saving inspiration, tracking data, and collaborating across teams often mean juggling multiple tools and losing context along the way.

With Cluing, you get a **central workspace** to easily **collect**, **organize**, **analyze**, and **collaborate** on key content, making your social media marketing efforts more efficient and effective.

## Save Your Inspiration Instantly

Scrolling through LinkedIn, X, Reddit, TikTok, or Substack and spotting that perfect creative spark? Heard something interesting in a YouTube video?

![](/media/2544230702/image.webp)

With Cluing, you can save inspiring posts directly into your workspace - on desktop or mobile - without switching apps or losing that idea in the feed.

**On Desktop:**

-   Use the **[[articles/11839482-how-to-install-the-cluing-extension-and-create-your-first-topic|browser extension]]** to save posts, highlights, or videos as soon as you see them - one click captures the full content, not just a link.

-   For platforms without a direct Save button (like TikTok or Substack), use **[[articles/11784694-capture-content-from-social-media-on-desktop|Add Link]]** - paste the URL and Cluing captures it as a snippet.

-   Found a long article or competitor post? Use **[[articles/14211688-summarize-webpage|Summarize Webpage]]** to let AI extract the key points and save them automatically - no need to read everything word for word.

![](/media/2544218708/image.webp)

**On Mobile:**

-   Install the **[[articles/13833323-cluing-mobile-apps|Cluing app]]** (iOS and Android) and use the native **Share** **button** from any app - TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Substack - to send content straight to your Cluing Inbox.

**Organize as you go:**

-   **Custom Tags:** Mark your saves with [[articles/11782904-how-to-add-tags-to-snippets|tags]] like #inspiration, #visualideas, or #contenttrends for easy retrieval later.

-   **Organized Storage:** Arrange saved content into Topics dedicated to different campaigns, clients, or themes.

## Collect and Organize Relevant Insights

Successful social media marketing requires keeping track of trends, audience engagement, and industry insights. Cluing provides a structured way to collect and store valuable information for easy access and collaboration.

![](/media/2544219117/image.webp)

-   **Centralized Content Repository:** Create Topics where you save articles, case studies, competitor activity, and audience feedback - all in one place, linked to their original source.

-   **Manual Curation:** Hand-pick and store insights relevant to your marketing goals instead of relying on scattered bookmarks or notes.

-   **Tag and Filter:** Label your insights with #engagement, #trendingtopics, or #competitorwatch to keep everything structured and easy to find.

-   **AI Organization:** Too many snippets to sort manually? In **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]**, ask the Workspace Organizer to sort everything into the right Topics for you.

## Stay Ahead of the Competition

Keeping an eye on your competitors is critical in the fast-paced world of social media. With Cluing, you can build a dedicated Topic to track competitor campaigns, saving posts, ads, and content analyses in one place.

-   **Competitor Snapshots:** Save and annotate competitor posts or ads for later review - the content stays in Cluing even if the original gets deleted.

-   **AI-Powered Analysis:** Once you've collected competitor content in a Topic, go to **[[articles/12606209-ai-chat-in-cluing|AI Chat]]** and @mention it directly in your prompt. Ask things like:

    -   _"Analyze @Competitor Content and tell me what topics they post about most."_

    -   _"What tone and messaging patterns repeat across @Competitor Content?"_

    -   _"Where are the content gaps I could own based on @Competitor Content?"_

-   **Collaborative Insights:** Invite team members to discuss competitor strategies directly in comments, ensuring you always have fresh perspectives to drive your content forward.

## Collaborate with Others

Social media success isn't just about data - it's about relationships. Cluing makes it easy to collaborate with influencers, clients, and subject-area experts by providing a shared space to exchange insights and feedback.

![](/media/2544224542/image.webp)

-   **Direct Collaboration:** Share Topics with influencers and experts using **[[articles/11793611-how-to-mention-collaborators-in-comments|@mentions]]**, allowing them to add their insights and feedback directly to your research.

-   **Client and Partner Input:** **[[articles/11793739-mention-external-users-by-email|Invite]]** brand partners, agencies, or internal teams to contribute relevant content for upcoming campaigns.

-   **Streamlined Feedback:** Collect and organize all suggestions in one place, making it easy to refine content before publishing.

-   **Slack Integration:** Connect your team's **[[articles/11776096-mentioning-users-from-slack|Slack]]** to share insights and tag collaborators wherever they work.

## From Insights to Content - in Canvas

Once you've gathered inspiration, tracked competitors, and collected team input, the next step is turning it all into something publishable. That's where **[[articles/12701026-ai-canvas|Canvas]]** comes in.

In AI Chat, **@mention any Topic** and ask Cluing to draft content directly from what you've saved:

-   _"Write 5 LinkedIn post ideas based on the trends in @Content Inspiration."_

-   _"Draft a campaign brief using the competitor insights from @Competitor Content."_

-   _"Turn the top themes from @Audience Feedback into content pillars for next month."_

The output opens in **Canvas** - Cluing's built-in editor - where you can refine, edit, and iterate with AI until it's ready. No switching to another tool, no losing context, no starting from scratch.

## Bringing It All Together

Here's what a full social media workflow looks like in Cluing:

1.  **Save** inspiration from LinkedIn, TikTok, Substack, X, and YouTube - on desktop or mobile - as you scroll.

2.  **Organize** it into Topics by campaign, client, or theme. Let AI sort what you don't have time to file manually.

3.  **Analyze** competitor content and audience trends using AI Chat and @mention.

4.  **Collaborate** with influencers, clients, and teammates in a shared workspace.

5.  **Create** - ask AI to turn everything into drafts, captions, or campaign briefs in Canvas.

Whether you're launching a new campaign, monitoring competitors, or brainstorming with influencers, Cluing keeps your research structured, your team aligned, and your content pipeline moving.
