Need a hard copy of your saved snippets? You can easily print a Topic - comments included – right from your Dashboard.

Here’s how:

1.  Go to your **Dashboard**

2.  Open the **Topic** that contains the snippets you want to print

3.  Click the **“More options”** (three dots) in the top right corner

4.  Choose **“Print”**

![](/media/2499504160/image.webp)

From there, you can either:

-   **Save as PDF** if you want a digital copy

-   Or send it straight to your **printer** for a hard copy

![](/media/1634143895/image.webp)

Simple and useful for sharing or archiving your research offline. 🙌🏼
