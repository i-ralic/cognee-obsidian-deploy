## Connecting the Slack Workspace

First is connecting your Slack Workspace with your Cluing Account. To do so follow these steps:

1.  Go to [Dashboard](https://app.collabwriting.com/)

2.  Open application settings by clicking your initials in the top-right corner

3.  Click "Add new Slack workspace" button

![](/media/1621181216/image.webp)

4\. Choose Slack Workspace you want to connect (top-right corner)

![](/media/1621181780/image.webp)

5\. Click **Allow** button

![](/media/1621182406/image.webp)

You’re all set - your Slack workspace is now connected. 🎊
